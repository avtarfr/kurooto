Check-ins and journal

At times you choose, Kurooto asks how your day is going. Your answers go into a journal in your notes, one daily note per day. Once a week you get your week in numbers, and in your first week a small daily quest helps you try things.

Check-ins

What it does. Up to three questions a day, each at its own time:

Check-inDefaultDefault question
evening22:00"How did today go? What got done, and what slipped?"
nightoff"Before bed: anything else for your journal? What's first tomorrow, and when should I wake you?"
middayoff"How's the morning been? Anything to re-plan for the afternoon?"

Reply to the message (Telegram's Reply) and your answer goes into today's journal: "Saved to your journal (journal/2026-11-12.md)". In the Android app it's a card under Waiting for you: type how it went and tap Send, or tap its buttons; with the computer asleep the answer waits on your call line (phone-app.md).

The day's reflection (checkins.reflect, on). The evening check-in message also lists up to three things the day's records show you did, and asks one thing about tomorrow:

Evening check-in: How did today go? What got done, and what slipped?

From today's records:
- Problem set 3: done
- send the glacier draft to Ortiz: done
- Studied Linear Algebra 1.5 h

What's first tomorrow?

Only records count, never a guess from what you wrote: a deadline marked done (Done, ticked in your notes, handed in on Classroom), an open thread closed as done, progress you gave on a deadline ("Essay draft: 4 of 7 done"), study time of 15 minutes or more for a subject, and classes whose after-class question you answered. In that order, three at most. Fewer happened: it lists what there is. Nothing recorded: no list at all, just the question. There's no streak, no score and nothing about what you didn't do. Not tonight under it ends the check-in for the day. Turn the reflection off with checkins.reflect (the message is then as before, with Skip today).

Turn it on. Features → Check-ins. It needs Telegram. It's in the ★ Starter set. To turn on the night or midday one, give it a time: Dashboard → Settings → Check-ins → times, e.g. "night": "23:15". Both are off until you do.

A day Kurooto can't pin down ("yesterday", "next week", a date) goes to your AI instead of being guessed. "call me" in the sentence is about a call, not a check-in.

It learns your times (checkins.learn_times, on). When, over the last two weeks, you answered a check-in on at least 5 days and on 4 or more of them 30 minutes or more away from its time, always on the same side (you moved it, or replied late but within 3 hours), its time moves to when you actually did it, to the quarter hour: "Your evening check-in is at 23:00 from now on, not 22:00: on 5 of the last 6 days you did it around then", with Undo. It isn't moved again for two weeks, and one you undid is left alone for a month.

The check-in day runs from 05:00 to 05:00 (checkins.day_starts). A night check-in at 00:30, or one you push past midnight, still belongs to the evening before: its journal entry and study logs go to that day. Still up at 1 am, "move the evening check-in to 21:00" means the coming evening, and "skip the night check-in" skips the coming night's once tonight's is over. Set it to 00:00 for plain calendar days. Once Kurooto has learned when you're usually up (calls.md, availability.learn_morning), the day turns two hours before that (14:00 if you're up around 16:00), unless you set it yourself.

Lighter days. The day after an exam or a heavy study day (notify.calm.lighter_after), the day's first check-in comes an hour later. Not today under the morning line puts it back (messages-it-sends.md).

On the dashboard. The Today page lists today's check-ins with their state, +30 min (pushes it back half an hour, like In 30 min on Telegram) and skip.

Limits and edge cases.

  • The computer was off at check-in time: if it's back within 2 hours, the check-in comes then. Later than that, you get "I missed your evening check-in (the computer was off). Reply here any time and I'll put it in your journal."
  • Focus time holds a check-in message until focus ends. Paused: it comes when you resume.
  • Calm counts check-in messages against the daily limit; past it they wait for the evening bundle.
  • With checkins.journal off, replies are only thanked, not saved.

What leaves your computer. Nothing for message check-ins (your reply travels through Telegram to your own bot). By call, see below.

Turn it off. Features → Check-ins → Turn off. One check-in only: empty its time in checkins.times. Your journal stays.

Check-ins by call

With Phone calls on and checkins.by_call on, each check-in is a phone call instead of a message.

Evening debrief (5 to 8 minutes): how the day went; what each of today's classes covered (with the after-class questions you kept for this call); what slipped from today's plan; open questions; the study time Kurooto counted, and study away from the computer; a short quiz if a quiz or exam is in the next two weeks. With checkins.reflect on, it says back in one line what the day's records show you did (the same list as the message).

Night check-in (3 to 5 minutes, if you turned it on): anything more for the journal, study logs still due, tomorrow's top priority and when to wake you (it sets the alarm), goodnight. With the night check-in off, the evening one asks tomorrow's priority and the wake-up time.

Midday check-in: a short call about the morning and the afternoon's plan.

Afterwards. With Chat on (and checkins.ai_notes on), the AI writes your journal entry from what you said, in your words, not its own, and a study log for each class you talked about. Without Chat, what you said goes into the journal as it is.

When it can't ring now. A check-in call never interrupts a class, a calendar event, /hold, focus, the night or another call. It waits until that ends ("Your evening check-in call waits until 18:02 (class: Linear Algebra)"), or, if that would push it into the next day, comes as a message. The night itself doesn't hold the night check-in (it's the goodnight call).

Too tired? Say so on the call, or tap Tomorrow morning: the rest moves to your wake-up call, which starts with it ("anything for Tuesday's journal? What did Linear Algebra cover?") before planning the day. With no alarm set, you get a message in the morning instead ("Last night's check-in, as promised: ...") at the end of your night hours (08:00 if you have none).

"Call me back in an hour" on a check-in call moves that check-in (the same call, with the same steps), never a plain reminder call. Past tonight, a night check-in goes to the wake-up call. Later on the ringing phone does the same, half an hour on (calls.later_min).

Missed or declined. A call you don't answer (after its repeats) or decline becomes a message with Call me now, Tomorrow morning and Skip; reply to it to answer by text. A call with no outcome after 15 minutes becomes that message too, so a check-in never stays "calling".

Gone to bed. With the Morning alarm set, the night check-in doesn't ring once it's past your bedtime (alarm.bedtime), or within 3 hours of you setting the alarm yourself. It moves to your wake-up call, and a message says so ("Your alarm is set, so I took it you're asleep: the night check-in moves to your wake-up call.") with Call me now and Skip. A night check-in you moved after setting the alarm still rings. Turn this off with checkins.asleep_defers.

The daily call limit. Calm allows one call a day that comes by itself (notify.calm.calls). Past it, a check-in comes as a message ("today's call came already"). /calm calls 2 allows two.

While the computer is off. Your call line knows today's check-in calls and rings them from what it last heard.

What leaves your computer. The call audio goes to Google (Gemini Live, your key) as for any call. With Chat on, what you said goes to the AI you picked to write your journal and study logs.

Turn it off. checkins.by_call off: check-ins come as messages again.

Your journal (the daily note)

Every day has one note in your notes folder:

Notes layoutWhere
kurooto (default)journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md
zettelkastendailies/YYYY-MM-DD.md
your ownnotes.paths.daily, e.g. diary/{date}.md

Kurooto only ever adds sections at the end; what's already there stays as it is. What goes in:

SectionFrom
## Evening check-in (22:04) (and night, midday)your reply to a check-in
the AI's journal entrya check-in call, with Chat on
## Journal 14:30the dashboard's journal box or Ctrl+K journal ...
## Plan for todaythe plan drafted before your alarm and settled after the wake-up call (calls.md)
## Study hoursthe study tracker, at 23:45
links to lecture notes and mail digestsstudy logs, the mail digest
your words, tasks ticked, ideas filedthe /journal skill (skills-and-routines.md)

Writing in it.

  • Reply to a check-in.
  • /journal <your day>: the skill saves your words as written, links the notes you mention, ticks tasks you finished and files new ideas in inbox/ (needs Skills and Chat).
  • The dashboard's Today page: the Journal box (Ctrl+Enter saves), or j had a good lab today in its main box, or Ctrl+K journal ... anywhere on the board.
  • Edit the note yourself, in any editor (Obsidian works).

The weekly summary counts journal days, and the nightly look back reads today's note unless you turn that off (retro.journal).

Weekly summary

What it does. Every Sunday at 19:00 (weekly.when), your week in numbers:

  • messages to Kurooto and AI requests answered (and failed);
  • reminders set and delivered;
  • check-ins answered out of those asked, and skipped;
  • deadlines marked done and nudges sent;
  • quiz answers and the average grade;
  • journal days out of 7;
  • lines from other features: study hours per subject (Study tracker), research activity, crunch weeks and what to start next week (Start-by dates);
  • Needs a look: what isn't working and could wait until Sunday (your-computer.md).
  • People (weekly.people, on): one line about the people in your project notes, only from what's logged there (minutes, "Last met", "Meets:"): who you met this week, who's next in the coming week, and anyone you meet regularly but haven't met for three weeks. "People (from your project notes): met Dr Okafor (guide, Glacier drift) Tue. next: Mira Castell Mon 10:00." It's never a message of its own, and nobody is mentioned from chats or mail.

/week shows it any time (the last 7 days). Everything is counted from Kurooto's own event log on this computer.

Sunday review call (optional; needs Phone calls; weekly.call on): after the summary, a call of about 15 minutes: the week in numbers and study hours per subject, what went well and what didn't, anything Kurooto should do differently, next week's deadlines and your priorities. Afterwards it becomes a weekly review in your journal (written by the AI with Chat on, your own words without). If the computer was off all Sunday evening (more than 3 hours past the time), you get only the text.

Turn it on. Features → Weekly summary. It needs Telegram.

Settings that matter.

SettingDefaultEffect
weekly.whenweekly sun 19:00when it comes (weekly <day> <HH:MM>)
weekly.calloffthe Sunday review call after it
weekly.peopleonthe People line from your project notes

What leaves your computer. Nothing but the message itself. With the call, the call audio goes to Google, and with Chat on, the transcript goes to your AI to write the review.

Turn it off. Features → Weekly summary → Turn off (or Less of this → Stop on a summary). With it off, health problems that could wait come as one message on Sundays instead.

First-week quests

What it does. For your first seven days, one short message a day at 18:00 with one thing to try. Each leaves something real behind. The quests, as your features allow:

QuestNeedsWhat it suggests
timetableSubjectsadd tomorrow's classes: /class tue 09:00-09:55 la
deadlineDeadlinessend one real deadline: "I have problem set 3 due fri 17:00"
reminderReminders"remind me at 21:00 to pack my bag"
doubtDoubt queue"I have a doubt about Linear Algebra: ..."
quizQuiz"quiz me on Linear Algebra"
studyStudy tracker/study la 25
check-inCheck-insanswer tonight's check-in in a line or two
rememberRemember"remember that I prefer short answers"
journalSkills/journal what I did today

Rules.

  • Only features that are on and pass their checks (what kurooto doctor looks at) get a quest; one you've already done (there's a timetable, you set a reminder this week) is left out. A day with nothing to suggest sends nothing.
  • A quest is done when the thing happens. Replying to the quest message with it works too: "remind me at 21:00 to pack my bag" as a reply sets the reminder.
  • Skip this one: tomorrow brings another.
  • Stop these, or a reply of "stop", "no more" or "no": no more quests, for good.
  • Leaving a day's quest alone (not done, not skipped, not answered) also stops them for good. No nagging.
  • No AI is involved and nothing leaves your computer.

Turn it on. Features → First-week quests (in the setup page's Features step or the dashboard).

From Telegram. /quests shows how far they got (and why they stopped, if they did). /quests stop ends them.

Settings. quests.when (daily 18:00), quests.days (7).

Turn it off. Features → First-week quests, or /quests stop. What the quests had you add (classes, deadlines, reminders) is yours and stays.

When something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
No night check-inIt's off until you give it a time: Settings → Check-ins → times, e.g. "night": "23:15".
A night check-in I didn't wantEmpty its time in checkins.times, or /checkin night skip for today.
The night check-in didn't ringThe alarm was set and it was past your bedtime: it moved to the wake-up call (a message said so). checkins.asleep_defers off makes it always ring.
The evening check-in didn't list something I didIt lists only what was recorded that day (a deadline marked done or given progress, a thread closed, 15+ minutes of study, an after-class answer), three at most. Mark the deadline Done, or /threads done N.
A check-in call came as a messageThe day's one call (notify.calm.calls) had come already, or a class, event, /hold or the night would have held it into the next day. /calm calls 2 allows two calls.
"skip Friday's check-in" went to the AIKurooto acts by itself only for today, tomorrow, the day after or a weekday name.
"I missed your evening check-in"The computer was off for more than 2 hours past the time. Reply to that message and it still goes into the journal.
My reply wasn't savedReply to the check-in message itself (Telegram's Reply), and check checkins.journal is on.
No Sunday review callweekly.call off, Phone calls not set up, or the computer was off more than 3 hours past the summary's time.
The quests stoppedYou tapped Stop, replied "stop", or left a day's quest alone. /quests says which.
