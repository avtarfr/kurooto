FAQ

Short answers, with a link to the full story. Something not working? See Troubleshooting.

Before you install

What is Kurooto, in one line? A personal assistant that runs on your own computer, talks to you through your own Telegram bot, and can ring your phone. See the front page.

Does it cost anything? No, unless you choose a paid plan: a paid Gemini key, or an AI plan you already pay for (Claude Code needs a paid Claude plan). Telegram, Cloudflare (for calls), GitHub (for Notes sync), Google Cloud (for Google features) and the Gemini free tier are used within their free plans.

Do I need a powerful computer or a graphics card? No. Without a graphics card the AI runs in the cloud and everything works; only the optional local models need a graphics card or an Apple Silicon Mac. About 1 GB of disk for Kurooto itself, plus what the features you pick need (the wizard shows the total). See Your computer.

Which computers? Windows 10 or 11, macOS 12 or newer, and Linux. No admin password is needed. See Getting started.

Do I have to be a student? No. The study features (subjects, timetable, quizzes, books) are off until you turn them on. Reminders, check-ins, the journal, notes, calls, the alarm, research digests and Google features are for anyone.

Do I need to know how to code? No. Everything is done through Telegram, the dashboard and the setup page. The terminal commands and add-ons are optional.

Which AI should I pick? The setup page marks Antigravity (agy) with ★: it's free with a Google account and has higher limits than a free key. A Gemini API key is the simplest, it's what Google recommends for tools like this, and phone calls, voice notes and the alarm need one anyway; its free tier runs out quickly, so set the other as a fallback. See AI brains.

Everyday use

Do I have to learn commands? No. Plain English works for most things ("remind me at 9 to call mum", "what's due this week?", "wake me at 6:45"). Commands are there when you prefer them; type / in the chat for a menu. See Commands.

Why didn't it understand me? Short everyday requests are understood instantly; anything else goes to your AI, which needs the Chat feature on. If Kurooto got it wrong, tap Wrong and teach it your words. See Teaching Kurooto.

Can someone else talk to my bot? No. It answers only the Telegram account that tapped the pairing link; everyone else is ignored.

Will it send email or messages for me? Never by itself. It sends nothing to anyone but you. A reply to an email waits in Gmail until you tap Send; approvals are never given for you.

It messages me too much. How do I quiet it? Tap Less of this under a message (Stop, Fewer or Another time), or lower the daily limit with /calm 4. At most 6 messages and 1 call a day come by themselves by default; the rest wait for one evening bundle. /why says why the last one came. See Messages Kurooto sends by itself.

How do I stop it calling me at a bad time? It never calls during a class on your timetable, a calendar event, the night (23:30 to 07:00 by default, or bedtime to your alarm), focus or another call. For a while: "no calls for 2 hours" or /hold 2h; /calls lifts it. See Phone calls.

Can I pause it? /pause, /pause 2h or /pause until 18:00. Telegram still answers, keeps what you send and does it when you /resume. Your call line still rings scheduled calls, so the alarm works. See Talking to Kurooto.

Your computer

Does it work when my computer is off or asleep? Partly. Telegram messages wait until it's back (Telegram keeps them for about a day). With Phone calls set up, calls and the wake-up alarm still ring from your call line, and you can answer questions in the phone app. With Reminders with the lid shut, your Android phone gets reminders at their time. Anything else is done when the computer is back, and Kurooto tells you it's back online. See Phone calls and Messages Kurooto sends by itself.

What happens on battery? By default (power.on_battery = saver) Telegram, reminders, check-ins, deadlines, calls and answers keep working; heavy work (local models, the notes index, backups, book reading, the nightly look back) waits for the charger and runs the moment you plug in. See Your computer.

Can I use it on two computers? One at a time per Telegram bot and call line. Notes sync keeps your notes in step; Profile sync moves your setup to a new computer. Turn Kurooto off on the old one (kurooto off). See Updates and backups.

How do I move to a new computer? With Profile sync on: install on the new one and press I've used Kurooto before on the setup page. Without it, a backup zip and kurooto restore. See Updates and backups.

Phone

Android or iPhone? Both. Android is best: calls ring like real phone calls, even on silent and on the lock screen. On iPhone, Kurooto is a Home Screen web app; calls arrive as notifications you tap. See Phone app.

Why isn't the Android app on the Play Store? It's installed from your own computer's pairing page. Android asks you to allow installing it; Play Protect may warn first ("Install anyway"). See Phone app.

I lost my phone. Run kurooto rotate on your computer: your call line gets new keys and forgets every paired phone. Then pair your other phones again. See Phone app.

Notes

Where are my notes? In a normal folder, Documents/Kurooto by default, as Markdown files. Open it in Obsidian or any editor. See Notes and the concept graph.

Will Kurooto change what I wrote? No. It adds notes and lines, and keeps its own parts between markers. When you ask it to edit a note, it changes one exact passage and keeps the previous version first.

Can I keep my notes in sync across devices? Yes, with Notes sync and a free private GitHub repository. Edits on two devices at once are both kept.

Privacy and data

Does my data go to whoever made Kurooto? No. Nothing is ever sent to Kurooto's author. What leaves your computer goes to the services you chose, under your own accounts. See Privacy.

What does the free Gemini tier mean for my data? Google may use what you send on the free tier to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Use a paid key if that matters (it's required in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). See AI brains.

Does it read all my email? No. The mail digest reads only the senders, domains or labels you choose; the rest is only counted. Mail marked confidential is never read. See Google.

How do I remove everything? kurooto uninstall --revoke deletes your call line and takes back Google's and the bot's access first; then delete the tokens and keys it lists. Your notes folder is never deleted. See Updates and backups.

Updates

Does it update itself? Yes, at night (03:00 to 05:00) by default, only signed releases, and it goes back to the previous version if a new one keeps crashing. updates.mode = ask asks first. See Updates and backups.

Can I try new versions early? Yes, the beta channel. See Getting started.

Getting help

Something's wrong. What do I send to the person helping me? kurooto doctor says what's wrong; kurooto doctor --bundle makes a zip with secrets, names, addresses and folders blanked out and without your messages or notes. Look inside it, then send it yourself. See Your computer and Troubleshooting.

Where's the code? On GitHub, under the Apache-2.0 licence. The README links it.
