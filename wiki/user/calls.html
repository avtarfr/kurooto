Phone calls and the morning alarm

Kurooto can ring your phone, and you can ring it. Calls go through your own call line, a small free program on your own Cloudflare account, and the voice is Google's Gemini Live with your own Gemini key. Calls keep working while your computer is asleep or off, from what it last sent.

One call at a time (0.7.3): Kurooto won't start a call while another of its calls is going on or ringing, and the phone app doesn't ring over a call you're already on; the second call waits and rings again soon. A wake-up alarm always rings.

This page covers calls, the morning alarm and reminders with the lid shut. Pairing and using the phone itself is on Your phone.

How calls work

What it does. Three pieces work together:

PieceWhat it is
Your computerRuns Kurooto. It keeps one outgoing connection to your call line (works behind any home or campus network; nothing on your computer listens to the internet) and answers what calls ask for: your notes, reminders, deadlines.
Your call lineA Cloudflare Worker named kurooto- plus random letters, at a workers.dev address, on your own free Cloudflare account. It rings your phone, hosts the call page, and keeps a short brief so calls work while the computer is off. The address is public but useless without the keys only your computer and paired phones have.
Your phoneThe Kurooto Android app, or the Kurooto web app on an iPhone's Home Screen, or any browser with a call link. See Your phone.

During a call the audio goes between your phone and Google (Gemini Live, your key). Only tool requests ("what's due?", "add a reminder") come back to your computer.

Every 5 minutes, and whenever your scheduled calls change, your computer sends the call line what it needs while the computer is off: how to talk, a brief (what's due, today's classes and check-ins, open questions), and the calls booked for the next 24 hours (up to 30).

Set up Phone calls

What it needs.

  • A Gemini API key (free from Google AI Studio). If your main AI is something else, the setup asks for one; calls, voice notes and the alarm always use it. See AI brains.
  • A free Cloudflare account. You can make one during setup.
  • About 350 MB of disk, once: Node.js (about 120 MB, inside Kurooto's own folder) and the call line's packages (about 230 MB).

Turn it on.

  1. Dashboard → Features → Phone calls → paste your Gemini key if it asks → Set up (or tick Phone calls in the setup wizard).
  2. Kurooto installs what it needs (with progress), then opens Cloudflare's sign-in in your browser. Sign in or make an account, and approve. Kurooto uses its own Cloudflare login, kept apart from any other Cloudflare login on your computer.
  3. Kurooto makes your workers.dev subdomain if you don't have one, creates the call line's keys, deploys it, checks it answers, and connects.
  4. Pair your phone: Your phone.

Setup is safe to repeat. If the Cloudflare sign-in didn't finish, press Set up again; the sign-in link is also in the progress log.

After Kurooto updates itself, it checks at 05:10 whether the call line's code changed and redeploys it in the background. If that fails you get "Your call line couldn't be updated: ... (calls keep working on the old version; Settings → Features → Phone calls → Set up tries again)." This doesn't happen when updates.mode is off.

Calling Kurooto

From Telegram: /call sends a Call now button with a link. It works from any phone or browser for 2 hours.

From the phone app: the Android app and the iPhone Home Screen app have a Call button.

What happens: Kurooto picks up, starts by looking at what needs your attention (open questions first), and talks like a friend on the phone. Talk in any language Gemini Live speaks; it follows yours.

Past that, a check-in comes as a message, and any other call waits in the evening bundle with a Call now link (valid for 26 hours). Calls you asked for, the alarm, the exam leave-by alarm and a deadline less than 12 hours away never count and are never held. /calm calls 2 allows two. See Messages Kurooto sends by itself.

When calls are held

A call that comes due when you shouldn't be disturbed waits, and you're told: "Held a call until 18:02 (class: Linear Algebra): the essay". It's placed by itself when the block ends.

What holds callsDetails
Another callNothing else rings while you're on a call (also when the computer is off). Updates wait too.
/hold 18:00, /hold 2h, "no calls for an hour"Until then. /calls or "calls are fine again" lifts it. Messages still come.
Focus/focus 2h, or a calendar event with #focus. Calls held and non-urgent messages wait.
The nightavailability.night, 23:30 to 07:00. With the alarm set, the night is from your bedtime until the alarm instead (see Morning alarm).
A classEvery class on your timetable, plus 5 minutes before it (subjects.margin_min), while subjects.no_calls_in_class is on. A class you /skip doesn't hold calls. Holidays, exam periods and days after the term ends have no classes.
A commitmentEach of your commitments (the gym, a shift, a standup) while it's on, unless you skipped it today or set /busy calls <name> off; a night shift holds calls until it ends.
A calendar eventEvery timed event (calendar.no_calls_in_events), or only events tagged #nocall and events in a calendar whose name contains "no call" when calendar.nocall_events = tagged.
An examWith Exam season on and its no_calls part on: from 15 minutes before an exam until exam_season.exam_min (180) minutes after it starts.

Never held: the morning alarm and exam leave-by alarms (they ring through the night and holds), "call me now", and kurooto call --force. A repeat ring of an unanswered call is dropped (it becomes a missed call) if a block started meanwhile.

Check-ins wait out a block and ring when it ends, or come as a message if that would be too late. See Check-ins.

On a call

  • Speak naturally. You can interrupt it, as on a real call.
  • Interrupt button: talk over it when the loudspeaker's echo keeps cutting it off.
  • Mic gate slider: needs a louder voice before it counts as speech. Move it right in a noisy room.
  • Earpiece / Speaker: switches output. Some browsers can't switch; use earphones then. In the Android app the screen goes off at your ear.
  • Mute, Hang up.
  • Open questions and approvals come first. Every call starts with what needs you (pending_items): open questions, approvals, deadlines soon, today's classes and check-ins, calls booked. Answer them by voice. An approval that arrives during a call is asked right away.
  • "Call me back in an hour" during a check-in or the wake-up call moves that same call (with its steps), not a new reminder call. On the wake-up call it works like a snooze.
  • Long jobs: ask for something that needs real work ("write up a plan for the lab report") and it hands it to the AI on your computer (run_task, needs Chat). It says it's started and keeps talking; the result is told on the call when it's ready, or sent to Telegram as "From your call: ..." if the call ended.
  • Your persona: calls.persona adds words on how to talk, e.g. "be brief and a bit cheeky".

The call's transcript is kept on your computer (Kurooto's state folder → calls/transcripts/, readable only by your user account). A check-in's transcript becomes your journal and study logs; the wake-up call's becomes today's plan.

When the voice service is down

Google's live voice sometimes refuses calls ("503: the service is currently unavailable"). The call still happens, in one of three ways, tried in order:

  1. Live again. The call page shows "Reconnecting…" and tries after about 1 second, then 3, each time with the next Live model: calls.live_model, then calls.live_models. A call that drops in the middle is tried again once.
  2. Turn by turn. "The live voice isn't working, so we'll take turns." Your phone's own speech recognition hears you, a text AI answers, and your phone reads the answer aloud. Tap Talk if it stopped listening; tap while it speaks to skip the rest. Same call, same steps, same tools. Your computer answers each turn (your Gemini key, or the model on your computer with calls.turn_brain = local; never agy or Claude Code, which are too slow to start). If the computer is off or doesn't answer within 25 seconds, Gemini on your call line answers.
  3. Text on Telegram. When your phone can't turn speech into text, or there's no microphone: "Your call couldn't go on by voice (...), so let's carry on here." The call goes on as messages, about the same thing. Reply or just type; what you type is part of the call, not a new request. End the call ends it, and so do 20 minutes without a message ("Ended the call (no messages for 20 minutes)."). Without Telegram, it goes to the phone app with a Reply box. If it happened while the computer was off, it starts on Telegram when the computer is back, within 2 hours; later than that you get "A call couldn't connect by voice while this computer was off ... Tell me here if it still matters."

Whichever way the call went, its transcript is kept and used the same way. kurooto doctor has a line "Phone calls: voice service": how often Live failed this week and what calls did instead, and it names a model in your list that Google says no longer exists.

Limits of turn by turn: the screen has to stay on (the app keeps it on; in a browser don't lock the phone or switch apps, or tap Talk again). The look back after every call

What it does. New in 0.5.8. After every call that said something (at least 4 lines), a stronger AI request (your main AI, at high effort) reads the transcript and the tools the call used, and:

  1. Does what slipped through. Anything you asked for that the call misheard, forgot, promised without doing, or where a tool failed: it does it now if it's safe to do unasked (a reminder, a note, a deadline, a journal line, an answer you wanted). Anything that sends a message, places a call, deletes or spends money it doesn't do: it lists it under "Needs you".
  2. Keeps lessons. Up to 3 short, general instructions for future calls ("When they say 'after class', ask when class ends before setting the reminder."). Every later call is told them. No names or details of other people, nothing secret.

The message. Only when something was fixed or learned: "After the call: <what it fixed>", "Needs you: ...", and "Learned for next time: ..." with a pointer to /lessons. A call where nothing slipped and nothing was learned sends nothing.

From Telegram:

CommandDoes
/lessonsLists what calls have learned, numbered.
/lessons forget 2Removes lesson 2 (remove 2 and drop 2 work too).
/lessons clearRemoves every lesson.

The lessons are a plain file you can edit: Kurooto's state folder → calls/lessons.md (one - line each). At most calls.lessons_max (12) are kept: a new lesson that says much the same as an old one replaces it, and the oldest go first.

Settings that matter (Dashboard → Settings → calls):

Limits. It needs a main AI set. It runs in the background lane, so it waits behind nothing you asked for, and it counts as background work for free limits (it can wait past 70% of a known daily limit; see AI brains).

What leaves your computer. The transcript and the call's tool results go to the AI you picked, like any request.

Turn it off. calls.review = false. Lessons already kept stay until you /lessons clear.

While your computer is off

  • Calls Kurooto booked still ring: your call line holds the next 24 hours of calls (reminder calls, check-ins, the alarm, leave-by alarms) and places them itself. When the computer is back: "While this computer was off, your call line called you: ...".
  • You can still call. The call says the computer is off and answers from the last brief: what's due, today's classes and check-ins, open questions, your notes' titles. Notes search is keyword-only; whole notes can be read only with Notes sync on (from your GitHub repository).
  • What needs the computer is either saved and done when it's back ("Saved. Their computer is off; it will do this as soon as it's back on.") or the call says it can't right now.
  • Answers you give on the phone wait on your call line and are used when the computer is back.
  • Paused (/pause): the call line keeps placing scheduled calls, so the alarm still rings.
  • Turned off (kurooto off): the call line stops placing scheduled calls and drops what it holds, until you turn Kurooto on again. kurooto off --keep-calls keeps them (for the alarm).
  • On battery: calls and the alarm keep working in every battery mode except pause. See Your computer.

Morning alarm

What it does. Kurooto wakes you with a call. On the Android app it rings with your alarm sound on the alarm volume, so it rings even on silent. Your call line places it, so it rings even if your computer is asleep or off.

Turn it on. Dashboard → Features → Morning alarm. Needs Phone calls.

On the evening check-in call: "wake me at 7". The /goodnight 7:00 skill sets it too. "Tomorrow" said at 2 am means the coming morning (the day runs from checkins.day_starts, 05:00).

The Today page has the alarm with a box ("7:30", "tomorrow 6:45"), skip tomorrow, off, and the daily alarm. See Dashboard.

How it rings:

  • Not answered: rings again 5 minutes later, up to twice more (alarm.retries).
  • Snooze on the ringing phone: 10 minutes (alarm.snooze_min). "Call me back in ten minutes" on the call does the same.
  • Several alarms: each one you set waits its turn, and the next takes over once one has rung. Your call line holds them all, so each rings even with this computer asleep. One you set for a morning replaces the daily alarm that morning.
  • The daily alarm is always with your call line well before the night: the next one is sent as soon as one has rung, at start, after the computer wakes, and checked every 30 minutes.

The night follows your alarm. While an alarm is set, no other calls from your bedtime (alarm.bedtime; empty means the start of the night hours, 23:30) until it rings, and once you're up calls can come even if the usual night hours aren't over. An alarm set for a nap counts from when you set it. Without an alarm, the night is the fixed hours (availability.night). With availability.night.alarm_only on, the fixed hours stop holding calls: only bedtime to the alarm does, and with no alarm set nothing holds calls overnight (a reminder call you set for 02:00 rings at 02:00). Off (the default) is the safe choice.

Night hours that fit you (availability.learn_night, on). Once a day Kurooto looks at the last three weeks of when you were around (your messages, buttons you tapped, calls you answered, "I'm awake") and finds each day's long quiet stretch. With 7 or more, the middle of when they start and end (to the half hour) are your usual quiet hours. If they're an hour or more from the night hours, the night hours move there, with one line and Undo: "Quiet hours at night (no calls) now run from 08:00 to 16:00, not 23:30 to 07:00: that's when you've been quiet on 12 of the last 21 days." Nights that run into the afternoon are fine. At most once a week; night hours you set yourself are never changed, and after Undo it leaves them alone for a month. Only those two times are kept: it's when you're around, nothing more.

Mornings move with them (availability.learn_morning, on), in the same line, unless you set them yourself: the check-in day turns two hours before you're usually up (checkins.day_starts), Dreaming's morning message comes half an hour after (dreaming.when), and Start-by's "Time to start" an hour after (startby.when). A night check-in put off to the morning already comes at the end of the night hours.

No alarm set? (alarm.suggest, on). Within the hour before your usual bedtime, with no alarm for the coming morning: "You usually wake around 16:30; set an alarm?" with Set 16:30 and Not tonight. At most once a day, and it never sets one by itself.

Your day, drafted. With Chat on, 20 minutes before the alarm (alarm.prep_min) the AI drafts today's plan into today's daily note under "## Plan for today (draft …)": classes, what's due, a top 3, suggested blocks, questions for you (the morning-prep skill). It runs once a day and never holds up your own requests.

The wake-up call goes through that plan, asks which classes you're skipping, what's due, start-by dates that have come, when to call today (the evening check-in, and a midday one if you want), and wishes you a good day. If last night's check-in was moved to this call, it starts with that. Afterwards, with Chat on, the AI settles "## Plan for today" in the daily note from what you said (the morning skill); without Chat, your words go into the daily note as they are (alarm.notes).

The morning page. At the alarm, if this computer is awake and has a screen, it opens http://127.0.0.1:8770/morning in your browser (alarm.open_page): today's plan, classes, calendar, what's due, check-ins and open questions.

"I'm awake". Say "I'm awake", "I just woke up" or "I'm up" (even with "call me if you need to ...") and Kurooto rings the morning call now, planning from the current time, and opens the morning page. It's a plain call, not the alarm: no snooze, no repeat rings. If an alarm is still set within 12 hours, it says so ("cancel my alarm" turns it off).

Keep this computer awake until the alarm (alarm.keep_awake, off): from 12 hours before the alarm until 15 minutes after it rings (or until you answer), the computer doesn't sleep by itself, so the plan is drafted in time. Linux: it keeps running with the lid closed too. macOS: a closed lid still sleeps a Mac (unless an external display is attached). Windows: the lid follows Power Options. On battery, setting the alarm says "Plug this computer in: it stays awake until then." The hold ends by itself, even if Kurooto stops; the screen can still turn off.

Limits and edge cases.

  • A skipped day can't be un-skipped; set that morning's alarm yourself ("wake me at 7 tomorrow").
  • The plan needs the alarm set at least a few minutes ahead, Chat on, and the computer awake then.
  • Every night at 21:00 Kurooto's health pass checks tonight's alarm is planned, on your call line, and that a phone is paired. If not, at once: "Your alarm at 07:00 might not ring: ... Set your phone's own alarm too." with a Fix button. See Your computer.

What leaves your computer. The alarm time goes to your call line. With Chat on, the AI reads your notes to draft the plan, and the wake-up call's transcript to settle it.

Turn it off. Features → Morning alarm → Turn off. alarm.keep_awake off lets the computer sleep at once.

Reminders with the lid shut

What it does. When your computer is asleep or off (a shut lid, a flat battery, kurooto stop), your Android phone still gets your reminders at their time, and in the morning one line with the day's first class and what's due: "First class 09:00: Linear Algebra. Due today: Problem set 3 (17:00)."

Turn it on. Dashboard → Features → Reminders with the lid shut. Needs Phone calls and the Kurooto app on an Android phone (open it once after updating so it makes its key). An iPhone can't get these.

How it works.

  • Whenever your reminders change, every 10 minutes, and as the computer goes to sleep (Linux and Windows say so), Kurooto hands your call line what's timed in the next day (up to 40 items), each sealed with a key only your phone has. The call line keeps the times, not the text.
  • Whichever side is awake says it, once. Computer awake: Telegram as usual. Asleep: the call line sends it to your phone (through ntfy.sh, still sealed) as a normal notification, and the computer doesn't repeat it when it wakes. Telegram doesn't get reminders while the computer sleeps; they come when it wakes, unless your phone already had them.
  • The morning line comes with your alarm if one is set that morning, else at lidsafe.morning (07:30), never later than 30 minutes before the first class. It only comes from the call line: when the computer is awake, the morning page and check-ins have it.
  • Computer on but can't reach the call line when a reminder is due: it waits 2 minutes (30 just after waking), then says it anyway. If the call line sent it meanwhile, it comes twice.
  • macOS doesn't tell programs it's about to sleep: a reminder set in the seconds before you shut a Mac's lid may wait until it wakes.
  • A reminder the call line couldn't get to your phone (5 tries, a minute apart) comes on Telegram when the computer is back: "Your call line couldn't get this to your phone: ...".

Check it. kurooto doctor or /health: "Reminders with the lid shut: your call line holds 5 for 1 phone(s)". "No phone can open them yet": update the Android app and open it once.

What leaves your computer. The next day's reminder times and ids, and for each Android phone a copy sealed with its key, go to your call line; your phone's public key too. Sent ones pass through ntfy.sh, still sealed.

Turn it off. Features → Reminders with the lid shut → Turn off. Your call line drops what it holds at once (or the next time the computer reaches it).

Calls from a terminal or script

kurooto call "the backup finished" rings your phone. It waits out your no-call rules (classes, /hold, focus, the night) and calls when they end.

OptionDoes
--at 17:30, --at "in 20 min", --at "tomorrow 7:00"Calls later.
--forceRings now despite no-call rules (never during another call).
--alarmRings as a wake-up alarm (no-call rules don't apply).
--listThe calls booked for later, with their keys.
--cancel KEYTakes a booked call off (your call line forgets it too).

It needs Kurooto running (kurooto on); otherwise it says so and exits with 2, like kurooto send, notify and ask-me (1 means Kurooto refused the call, with the reason). More script commands are on Add-ons and scripts.

What leaves your computer

  • Call audio goes between your phone and Google (Gemini Live, your key). On the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products.
  • Turn by turn: your speech goes to your phone's speech service unless it's recognised on the phone; each turn's text goes to Gemini (your key) or the model on your computer.
  • Text on Telegram: the conversation goes through your Telegram bot and your AI, like your other messages.
  • Your call line, on your Cloudflare account, keeps: your Gemini key; a short brief (what's due, today's classes and check-ins, open questions, the steps for check-in, wake-up and Sunday review calls, your notes' titles); the calls booked for the next day; questions and messages waiting for your phones; and, only if you approved it, your bot's token (for "Kurooto has been offline since ..." messages; see Messages it sends).
  • Transcripts, lessons and everything else stay on your computer. The look back sends a call's transcript to your AI.
  • Full list: Privacy.

Turn it off

Turning Phone calls off keeps the calls you booked: they ring when it's back on. A call whose time passed while it was off doesn't ring late; Kurooto tells you it was missed instead.

  • Features → Phone calls → Turn off. Morning alarm and Reminders with the lid shut need it, so turn those off first.
  • Just the look back: calls.review = false.
  • Just turn by turn: calls.voice_mode = live (failed calls go to Telegram).
  • Keep calls from ringing for a while: /hold 2h, or /focus.
  • The call line stays on your Cloudflare account until you delete it (Cloudflare dashboard → Workers & Pages → the kurooto-… worker → Settings → Delete), or until kurooto uninstall --revoke deletes it for you. See Updates and backups.

Things a call can send or start

  • "Send that on Telegram", "tell me on Telegram": the call sends it to your chat (a summary, a list, a link). A call never just promises it any more.
  • "Sync now", "check Classroom now": the same as /sync all; the result comes on Telegram when it's done.
