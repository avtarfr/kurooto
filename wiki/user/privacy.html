Privacy: what stays and what leaves

Kurooto runs on your own computer. Nothing is ever sent to Kurooto's author: no analytics, no crash reports, no "phone home". Everything that leaves your computer goes to a service you chose, under your own account, and only for a feature you turned on. Every feature is off until you turn it on, and each one says on its card in Dashboard → Features what it sends where.

This page is the full list. Each feature's own page repeats its part under What leaves your computer.

The principles

  • It runs here. Your notes, journal, deadlines, reminders, call transcripts and settings live on your computer.
  • Nothing to the author. The only thing Kurooto fetches from its own release site is updates and the list of withdrawn versions, both checked against a signature. Nothing about you goes the other way.
  • Your own accounts. The AI, Telegram bot, call line (Cloudflare), Google project, GitHub repository and backup folder are all yours. Kurooto signs in to each as you; no middleman server holds your access.
  • Nothing goes to anyone but you without your tap. Kurooto never emails, messages or shares on its own. An email reply is sent only when you tap Send; a class pack you make comes to your own Telegram for you to forward; approvals are never given for you.
  • The AI sees only what a request needs. What you ask, the notes it reads to answer, what its tools return, and two short notes about you that go along with every request: the "In brief" part of your profile (teaching-kurooto.md) and your how-to-help-me note.
  • Some things are never read by any cloud AI: mail marked confidential, calendar events marked private (only their time is kept), and anything Kurooto refuses to keep (below).

What leaves, feature by feature

"The AI you picked" means the brain you chose (ai-brains.md): Google for a Gemini key or agy, Anthropic for Claude Code, the service you entered for an OpenAI-compatible one, or nothing at all for local models.

Features that send nothing

These work entirely on your computer: Reminders, Deadlines (with recurring quizzes, the not-interested list and exam mode), Start-by dates, Subjects and timetable, Doubt queue, First-week quests, Calendar (it only reads your calendar's private link; event descriptions are read only for #nocall, #focus and #private and aren't kept; private events are kept only as "Busy (private)", not even told to your AI), Study tracker (window titles are read and thrown away; only minutes per subject are kept; the idle check never reads what you type), Quick notes and prompts, Local AI models, Stay awake on charger, Space keeper, the reserve (Markdown copies of Kurooto's state for AI programs, in the state folder), notes brain timelines and weekly checkup, kurooto top, kurooto runlog. Sync everything sends only what each sync's own feature sends.

Free AI tiers and their terms

A free Gemini API key (Google AI Studio's free tier):

  • Google may use what you send and what it answers to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Don't say or type anything there you wouldn't want read.
  • You must be 18 or older.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you need a paid key (billing on).
  • Phone calls and voice notes always use Gemini, whichever AI answers your messages (a turn-by-turn call can use the model on your computer instead).

On a paid key Google doesn't use what you send to improve its products. Or keep those features off, or use local models where they're offered.

Antigravity (agy): Kurooto runs Google's own agy program on your computer, signed in as you, and never touches its sign-in. Google's answers differ on whether other tools may start agy unattended, so there is some risk to your Google account; for tools like Kurooto, Google recommends a Gemini key.

Claude Code and OpenAI-compatible services work under their own terms and your own account. Claude Code gets its reach for each request only; nothing is saved in its own settings.

  • Secrets (bot token, API keys, Google sign-ins, call line keys, calendar links) are in your system's keychain: Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or the Secret Service on Linux. Where there is none (and when Kurooto starts at boot on Linux, while the keychain is still locked), they're in secrets.json in the settings folder, readable only by your user account. They're never written to logs.
  • The state folder holds your check-ins, deadlines, AI requests and answers, call transcripts, the schedule, questions, the record of messages that came by themselves (calm.db, kept 45 days: when, why, the setting, what it was about, the first 300 characters, whether you tapped it), test cases (cases/), the calls' lessons (calls/lessons.md), and earlier versions of notes the AI edited (note-history/, the last 20 per file). With Learns from its own work on: the search index of past requests, calls and chats (learning/history.db), and the earlier versions of skills it changed (learning/before/). What's kept of conversations stays until you set how long: How long it's kept.
  • The data folder holds the About you reports (never synced), Classroom's downloaded files, books and their text, and confidential mail summaries (mail/confidential/, readable only by your user account).
  • Mail copies: the text of mail chosen for the digest only until the digest is written; the sender, subject and a short excerpt of digested mail for 30 days, so a reply draft can answer it.
  • The dashboard (http://127.0.0.1:8770) answers only this computer, needs a key only its own page gets, refuses other websites, and on Linux checks that each request comes from your own user account.

How long it's kept

What Kurooto keeps of your conversations stays until you choose otherwise. Set privacy.keep_days (e.g. 30) and it's forgotten after that many days, every night at 04:15 (or when the computer wakes, if it slept through it); 0, the default, keeps it until you /forget it:

/forget 7d forgets the last 7 days of all that now (also /forget 12h, /forget 2w), and says how many of each kind went. /forget all forgets everything, after a Forget everything button. privacy.keep_days = 0 keeps everything until you forget it.

What it never touches:

  • Your notes folder. A voice note's recording is saved in your notes' inbox, so it stays; delete it there.
  • Test cases you made, open questions, a call still going.
  • The event log (events.jsonl): times, counts and request names, never what you typed or what was answered. Features learn your rhythm from it over weeks.
  • Messages on Telegram or your phone: they're in those apps; delete them there.
  • Backup zips you made before.

Your call line holds a call's transcript only while the computer is off and hands it over when it's back, so nothing waits there to be forgotten.

Things Kurooto refuses to keep

  • Remember won't store health, money or password details: they'd travel with every AI request. It says so and keeps nothing. Skills are told to keep health, money and other people's private details out of your notes too.
  • Learns from its own work never keeps a fact about health, money, passwords or other people (a line naming a relative or friend, a phone number, an email address).
  • Wrong button and test cases never keep long messages, several requests in one, or anything about health, money or passwords. Cases you didn't review are forgotten after 30 days (cases.keep_days).
  • Mail marked confidential is never read by the AI you picked, never summarised by it, and never answered with an AI draft. Kurooto reads its text on your computer only to spot the marking, then drops it.
  • Private calendar events keep only their time.
  • Events (the activity log) record counts and request names, never what you typed or answered.

What the AI may read and write on your computer

  • Read: your notes folder; your Documents, Downloads and Screenshots folders; Kurooto's copies of Classroom and mail; the reserve. Hidden files and folders (like .git) are left out, and a link that leads elsewhere is refused.
  • Write: your notes folder, Classroom's course files folder, and (with add-ons on) your add-ons' skills/. Text files only. Documents and Downloads stay read-only.
  • Your text: it adds notes and lines; it changes existing text only when you asked, one passage at a time, after keeping the previous version.
  • The web: public pages, read only, a few MB at most; never this computer or your local network.
  • Everything it reads goes to the AI you picked, like the notes it reads to answer.

Details: ai-brains.md.

Removing your data

  • Conversations: /forget 7d or /forget all (How long it's kept).
  • One feature: turn it off (Dashboard → Features). Its data stays until you delete it; your notes are never deleted.
  • A paired phone: /phones, then /unpair <id>. A lost phone: kurooto rotate gives your call line new keys and forgets every paired phone (phone-app.md).
  • Google access: /google forget <name>, or myaccount.google.com/connections (google.md).
  • Leaving for good: kurooto uninstall --revoke first deletes your call line from your Cloudflare account, revokes each Google sign-in and clears your Telegram bot's webhook and menu, then removes Kurooto's keys from your keychain and Kurooto itself. Your notes folder is never deleted. It lists what's left for you: the GitHub token(s), the Gemini key, the Google Cloud project, Profile sync's kurooto-profile branch, the bot itself (/deletebot to @BotFather), backup zips in the folder you picked. Step by step: updates-and-backups.md.
