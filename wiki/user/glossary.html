Glossary

Words this wiki uses, in plain terms. Back to the wiki's front page.

WordWhat it means here
About you profileA note in your notes folder (about-me/profile.md) that Kurooto builds from what your other AI accounts know about you. Its In brief part goes along with every AI request. See Teaching Kurooto.
Add-onYour own extra features, skills or prompts, loaded from a folder you name. Optional, for people who write code. See Add-ons and scripts. Not the same as an optional feature such as Exam season, which is part of Kurooto and simply off until you turn it on.
agy (Antigravity)Google's AI agent program that runs on your computer. One of the AI models you can pick.
AI modelThe AI service that answers your messages and does tasks for Kurooto: a Gemini API key, agy, Claude Code, a local Ollama model, or another OpenAI-compatible service. See AI models.
API keyA long secret code a service gives you so a program can use it on your behalf. Kurooto keeps keys in your computer's keychain.
ApprovalA message that asks for your OK before Kurooto does something (Approve / Deny, or choices like Send / Edit / Discard). Nothing is ever approved for you.
Bot / Telegram botYour own Telegram account for Kurooto, made with @BotFather. It answers only you.
@BotFatherTelegram's official bot that makes new bots and gives you their token.
Brief (call brief)A short summary your call line keeps (what's due, today's classes, open questions) so calls work even while your computer is off.
Call lineA small program Kurooto puts on your own free Cloudflare account. Your phone talks to it for calls; it can ring your phone while your computer sleeps. See Phone calls.
CalmThe rules that keep Kurooto quiet: every message that comes by itself says why, and at most 6 messages and 1 call a day come by themselves. See Messages Kurooto sends by itself.
Case / test caseA plain-English request Kurooto got wrong (or didn't understand), kept so you can teach it what you meant. See Teaching Kurooto.
ChatThe feature that sends free text to your AI model. Without it, only commands and instant requests work.
Check-inA message (or call) at a time you choose asking how the day is going; your reply goes to your journal.
Class packA small file with a class section's timetable, term dates and recurring quizzes, which classmates import into their own Kurooto.
Claude CodeAnthropic's AI agent program. One of the AI models; needs a paid Claude plan.
CloudflareA company whose free plan hosts your call line. You make the account; it's yours.
Concept graphThe feature that reads a finished book and weaves its ideas into your notes as linked concept notes. See Notes and the concept graph.
Daily noteOne note per day in your notes folder (journal/<date>.md by default) that holds your journal, the day's plan, check-in answers and study hours.
DashboardKurooto's page in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8770, only reachable from your own computer. See Dashboard.
Deadline listEverything that's due, gathered from what you add, your notes, Google Classroom, your calendar, recurring quizzes and your term note.
Evening bundleOne message at 20:00 listing what waited because the day's limit was reached (or because you chose "Another time").
Exam modeTurns on by itself for a subject two weeks before its quiz or exam: more practice, calls know about it.
Exam seasonAn optional feature (off, in no preset) for the whole exam period: alarm checks, exam-eve questions, an alarm to leave, debriefs. See Quiz and exams.
Fallback brainA second AI that answers when your main one hits its limit (brain.fallback; or a list of them; with none picked, Claude Code and agy stand in for each other at a limit).
FeatureOne part of Kurooto you can turn on or off on the dashboard's Features page. Everything is off until you turn it on.
Fine-grained tokenA GitHub access key limited to one repository; Notes sync and Profile sync use one.
FocusDo-not-disturb: calls are held and messages that can wait are kept until it ends (/focus 2h).
GBrain / notes brainThe search index of your notes that lets /notes find things by meaning and gives your AI a memory of them.
Gemini / Gemini LiveGoogle's AI. A Gemini API key is one of the brains, and phone calls, voice notes and the alarm always use it; Gemini Live is its real-time voice, used for calls.
HoldNo calls until a time (/hold 2h); messages still come.
Instant requestA plain-English message Kurooto understands by itself, without asking an AI ("remind me at 9 to call mum"). See Commands.
IntentThe developer word for an instant request.
JournalYour own words about the day, kept in the daily note.
KeychainYour system's password store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service) where Kurooto keeps tokens and keys.
Lessons (call lessons)What Kurooto learned from looking back at a call; every later call is told them. /lessons lists them. See Phone calls.
Local models / OllamaAI models that run on your own computer (through the Ollama program). Private and offline, but weaker; mostly used for quick decisions. Need a graphics card or Apple Silicon.
Look back (nightly)Once a day the AI reads how the day went and suggests up to three small improvements, which you approve or not.
MCPModel Context Protocol, a standard way for AI agent programs to use another program's tools. kurooto mcp gives agent programs Kurooto's tools.
Night (night hours)23:30 to 07:00 by default (availability.night); no calls then, and messages that can wait wait. With the alarm set, the night runs from your bedtime to the alarm.
Notes folder (workspace)The folder of Markdown notes Kurooto reads and writes, Documents/Kurooto by default. Works with Obsidian.
OAuth clientYour own registration with Google (a small JSON file) that lets Kurooto sign in to your Google account without anyone else's server. See Google.
PairingConnecting your Telegram chat or your phone to Kurooto with a one-time link or QR code.
PauseKurooto stops working for a while but Telegram still answers, keeps what you send, and does it all when you /resume.
PresetA ready set of features in the setup wizard: Starter (Telegram, Chat, Reminders, Check-ins, Deadlines) or Everything.
Profile syncYour Kurooto setup, encrypted with your passphrase, kept in your private GitHub repository so a new computer is set up in a minute.
Quiet hoursTimes when Kurooto doesn't call or send messages that can wait: the night, focus, /hold, classes and calendar events (for calls).
Repository (repo)A folder kept with its full history on GitHub. Notes sync uses a private one.
Reserve, thePlain Markdown copies of Kurooto's state (alarm, deadlines, calendar, settings) that AI programs can read. Also: the end of the AI's free daily limit kept back for what you're waiting on.
RoutineA command or request that runs on a schedule (/every monday 9:00 /journal plan my week).
self.mdWhat Kurooto is running (version, AIs and models, features and their health, recent failures), kept for its AI to read before answering a question about Kurooto itself.
SettingA named option such as deadlines.ping_h, changed on the dashboard's Settings page. The part before the dot is the section.
SkillReady-made instructions for your AI, run as a command (/journal, /study-log, /pack). See Skills and routines.
Start-by dateThe day Kurooto estimates you should start working on a deadline, from its size and your free time.
State folderKurooto's own working files (schedule, history, logs). kurooto paths shows where it is.
Study packA zip of a subject's notes and course files, with a prompt, for you to give to another AI (/studypack).
Term noteA note with your weekly timetable and key dates that Kurooto reads live (/term note).
TesseractA free program that reads text from scanned pages (OCR). Kurooto can install it for books.
TectonicA small LaTeX engine Kurooto can install to turn revision packs into PDFs.
TokenA secret code that lets a program use an account (your Telegram bot's token, a GitHub token).
Turn by turnA way of calling where your phone's own speech recognition listens and you take turns, used when Google's live voice is down or when you choose it.
WebhookA setting that sends a bot's messages to a web address. Kurooto can't read a bot that has one; the setup page offers to remove it.
Zettelkasten layoutAn alternative way of arranging your notes folder (dailies/, zettel/, sources/, maps/). See Notes.
