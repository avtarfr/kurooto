Kurooto user wiki

Kurooto is a personal assistant that runs on your own computer. You talk to it through your own Telegram bot (and, if you like, by phone). It reminds you of things, keeps track of what's due, asks how your day went and writes it into your journal, searches your notes, reads your books into notes, and can ring your phone, including a wake-up alarm. An AI you choose answers your questions and does the bigger tasks. Every feature is off until you turn it on.

This wiki is the guide for people who use Kurooto. Read it top to bottom once and you'll know everything it can do and how to ask for it. /help on Telegram lists the commands of the features you have on; Commands lists all of them.

Some things are always there and aren't features you turn on: questions and approvals (/q), /status, /pause, /health, /calm and /why, /jobs, /aboutme, updates, and the "going offline / back online" messages.

Three rules Kurooto keeps

  • It sends nothing to anyone but you. Emails, replies and anything outward wait for your tap; approvals are never given for you.
  • Your own text is never reworded or deleted. Kurooto adds notes and lines; when you ask it to edit a note, it keeps the previous version first.
  • Nothing goes to whoever made Kurooto. What leaves your computer goes to services you chose, under your own accounts. Privacy lists it per feature.
