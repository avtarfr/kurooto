Troubleshooting

Find what you see in the left column; the right column says what to do. Start with these four, which answer most problems:

  1. /fixme on Telegram or the dashboard's terminal prompt, or kurooto fixme in a terminal: what isn't working, each with its row from this page, known issues of your version and a Fix button where Kurooto can fix it. It works by rules, with no AI, so it answers when the AI is what broke (kurooto fixme also with Kurooto stopped). /fixme the phone doesn't ring finds the rows for what you see; Ask my AI (or /fixme ai) has your AI explain, if it works.
  2. /health on Telegram (or in the phone app's chat): what isn't working, with Fix buttons (/health now also checks online services).
  3. kurooto doctor in a terminal: every check, one line each; kurooto doctor --fix <id> runs a fix.
  4. /why when a message surprised you, and /calm when one didn't come.

Still stuck? kurooto doctor --bundle makes a zip for someone helping you, with secrets, names, addresses and folders blanked out and none of your messages or notes. Look inside, then send it yourself. See Your computer.

Setup and Telegram

What you seeWhat to do
The setup page didn't openRun kurooto open, or go to http://127.0.0.1:8770/setup. If another program uses that port, Kurooto picks the next one (kurooto paths → web.json in the state folder names it).
The setup page says "Kurooto isn't answering"It stopped or is restarting: start it from your app menu or with kurooto on, then press the button again. What you typed stays.
The setup page says it's out of dateReload the page.
The setup page opens on a step I already didIt opens on the first step not finished: an AI that doesn't answer its test yet keeps the AI step open. Pick any step in the list, or kurooto setup about (any step's name) to open one.
"finish setup (2)" on the dashboard, or setup: … notes in kurooto doctorSteps you skipped for now. Click one in Features → Finish setting up (or kurooto setup telegram) to finish it; it leaves the list once it's done. They're notes, never problems.
Not enough spaceThe wizard says what needs it. Free some space, or pick fewer features.
"That doesn't look like a bot token"Copy the whole token from BotFather's message (it looks like 123456789:AAH…).
Telegram didn't accept the tokenCopy it again, or send /token to @BotFather to see it.
"That token was revoked"Get the new token from @BotFather and paste it on the setup page. Messages to you start again as soon as it works; no restart needed.
"Got a /start without the code"Send the whole /start … message the setup page shows, not just Start.
"This link expired"Pairing links last an hour: press Get a new link.
"This bot is connected to a web address (a webhook)"Press Disconnect it on the setup page.
"Another program is reading this bot's messages"Another Kurooto (an old computer?) uses the same bot: kurooto off there, or make a new bot.
The bot doesn't answerkurooto doctor. Is Kurooto running, or turned off (kurooto on)? Is the computer asleep (see Stay awake on charger)?
The bot says it's paused/resume. Or it's on battery with power.on_battery = pause: plug in, or choose saver.
Two computers both answer, or the bot misses messages after movingTurn Kurooto off on the old computer: kurooto off.
"X is off: Features → X → Turn on"That command belongs to a feature that's off. Turn it on on the dashboard's Features page.
"I can't answer free text yet"Turn on Chat (Features).
The AI doesn't answer, or something broke and you don't know what/fixme (or kurooto fixme): it needs no AI. /fixme <what you see> finds the rows on this page.
"that button is from a feature that's off now"Turn the feature back on, or ignore the old message.
Telegram's "/" menu is out of dateIt updates within a minute of a feature going on or off, and every 15 minutes for skills. Turning telegram.menu off and on sets it again.
"Voice notes are off"Turn on Voice notes (Features); it needs a Gemini key.
"can't read pictures" after a photo with datesThe AI you picked can't read photos (an Ollama model without vision). Settings → AI: agy, Claude Code, a Gemini key, or an Ollama model that reads pictures (gemma3) (Photo to dates).
kurooto ask --popup opens no windowThis Python has no Tk (on Linux install python3-tk or tk), or there's no display; kurooto ask works in a terminal. The key that opens it is set in your system's keyboard settings (Quick ask).
"Couldn't get that file" (a big file)Telegram lets bots fetch files up to 20 MB. Put the file into your notes or files folder yourself.
"… Telegram takes files up to 50 MB" when Kurooto sends you somethingTurn on Drive upload: bigger files then come as a Google Drive link.
I don't want TelegramPick The Kurooto phone app instead on the setup page's Chat step (it needs Phone calls), or turn Telegram off in Features once Phone calls is on: everything then comes to the app's chat (phone-app.md).
"X needs Telegram or the phone app (Phone calls) first"That feature sends you things: connect Telegram, or set up Phone calls and pair the app.
"Voice notes needs Telegram first" with the phone appVoice notes and Files into notes are about what you send the bot; they need Telegram itself. Share files to the app instead.
kurooto doctor says "talks to you: on this computer only"Neither Telegram nor Phone calls is on, so messages only pop up on this computer. Set one up (a note, not a problem).

The AI

What you seeWhat to do
The AI test fails on the setup pageThe panel says why. A Gemini key starts with AIza; agy and Claude Code need their sign-in window again; Ollama needs ollama serve running; another service's address usually ends in /v1.
"… isn't signed in any more"Setup page → AI → sign in again → Test it.
"It's at its usage limit"Free tiers have daily limits. The other AI program answers meanwhile if it's set up (no fallback picked, or brain.fallback; the receipt says "... hit its limit; ... answered"); otherwise wait, or set a fallback brain (brain.fallback). /usage shows how often it happens and who is waiting for a reset.
The AI says something wrong about Kurooto itself (a feature, its version, a limit)It answers from self.md in the reserve folder, written when something changes. Ask again, or /fixme for the rules' answer; kurooto reserve writes it now.
"Google's free limit for today is used up"It comes back at the time the message says. Set a fallback brain meanwhile; calls and reminders still work.
The Classroom digest, the look back or a research digest didn't comePast 70% of a limit Google named (or one you set), background work waits for the reset; a limit Kurooto only learned from its own count never holds work (quota.soft). /usage says how many requests wait. Raise quota.background_pct or set quota.on to false (in settings.json).
"The AI stopped without an answer"Rephrase it, or do that part yourself.
"No answer in time"Start the message with low:, or split the request.
"It got stuck"Send it again. A request with no progress for jobs.stuck_min (10) minutes is stopped.
"No internet connection right now: … waits" while you're onlineKurooto saw Telegram, your call line or two other services not answering. It runs once one answers again (within a minute or two); /helper says whether it still thinks the internet is down. local_helper.offline = false turns the waiting off.
The offline helper "is too slow here"Its speed test needs a one-word answer within local_helper.max_s (1.5 s). Close heavy programs and /helper test again, or leave it off: quick decisions stay with the rules.
The AI said it did something but nothing happenedTell it so; check /jobs. A small local model may not support tools and answers without them.
The AI didn't set the alarm or book the call I asked forOutside a call it asks first: tap Approve under "Your OK first" (/q lists it). Or say it directly: "wake me at 6:45", "call me at 5".
The setup page says agy "can't use Kurooto's tools yet"agy's settings file isn't plain JSON, or agy mcp add failed. Add mcp(kurooto/*) to permissions.allow in agy's settings.json yourself, then Test again.
An agent program says Kurooto's tools aren't runningKurooto is off or restarting: kurooto on, then ask again.
Building my profile failedIt's a big request: if the AI hit a limit, try again later or with a stronger AI (brain.main).
"There's no ## In brief section"Add that heading to your profile with a few lines about you; without it nothing goes along with AI requests.
Apply says "you have uncommitted changes in ..."A proposal for a git project is one commit on your branch, and it won't touch files you're still changing: commit or stash those files (git stash), then tap Apply again. Changes in other files don't matter.
Apply says a file "changed since this was proposed"You (or a sync) changed it after the AI made its proposal. Tap Change and say "redo it on the new version", or Discard.
Apply says the repository is "on no branch" or "in the middle of a merge"Check out a branch (git switch main), or finish the merge or rebase, then tap Apply again.
A proposal says "Checks didn't run: this computer has no sandbox"Checks run only in a sandbox that keeps them in the scratch folder with no network: install bubblewrap on Linux (sudo apt install bubblewrap, sudo pacman -S bubblewrap, sudo dnf install bubblewrap). On Windows they don't run.
"The AI doesn't help with <project> yet"/project help on <project> lets it; /project lists your projects.

Messages

A message came only in the evening bundle, or not at allMore than notify.calm.messages (6) came by themselves that day, it's exam season, or you chose Fewer, Another time or Stop for that kind. The first time a day the limit holds one, a line says so with Send them as they come today and Raise to 12; the bundle's first line says why they waited. /why on the next one says which; /calm lists what's turned down, with buttons to start it again. /calm off brings back the old behaviour.
Requests run at a different effort than before, the fallback AI answers first, or Start-by sizes changedDreaming tuned it from what was recorded (one change a night, said in the morning message). /dream policy shows what's tuned and why; the morning line's Undo puts it back, or remove the entry from brain.effort_map, brain.fallback_first or startby.sizes. dreaming.tune_effort, tune_fallbacks or tune_sizing = false stops that area.
A check-in or deadline call came as a message insteadThe day's one call that comes by itself (notify.calm.calls) had come already. /calm calls 2 allows two.
"Back online" every morningYour laptop sleeps at night. Raise notify.presence.after_min past your night, or set notify.presence.on to false.
No "going offline" when the laptop sleepsThat message comes from your call line: it needs Phone calls set up and your OK for it to use Telegram (notify.presence.line_telegram), or add "app" to notify.presence.channels.
"needs a look" on the dashboard, or in the weekly summary/health (or the Features page) says what, with a Fix button where Kurooto can fix it. A sign-in that ended needs you; the line says where.
"That question expired"It waited longer than ask.expire_hours (48); nothing was done. The asker asks again if it still matters.
I tapped a button and nothing happenedIt may have been answered elsewhere (on a call, in the app): /q shows what's still open.

The phone app

What you seeWhat to do
The phone didn't ring, and /health, /status or the log shows "429 Too Many Requests" from ntfyntfy.sh limits each internet address, and yours is shared (a campus or office). A free ntfy.sh account doesn't help: ntfy counts it by address like everyone else. Update the phone app to 0.7.0 or later: it gets calls and messages straight from your call line, and ntfy is only its fallback. Check that the dashboard's Phone tab says connected beside the phone; if it doesn't, tick Battery: unrestricted on the app's checklist and open the app once.
The app closes as soon as you open it (Android 8 and up, app 0.7.0)A bug in 0.7.0, fixed in 0.7.1: the new mark at the top of Today couldn't be drawn. Update Kurooto on your computer (kurooto update --now, or wait for tonight); the app then offers the new version, or install it from the pairing page. Nothing you set up is lost.
A call rang while I was on another callIf your phone is on a call, Kurooto (0.7.3 on) doesn't ring over it: a quiet note, and it rings again after its usual wait. Before 0.7.3 it did. Kurooto's own calls also wait for each other. A wake-up alarm always rings.
My message got "On it" a minute late, or the journal says it failed0.7.3 reads Telegram on its own threads and answers what you type before automatic work. If agy is the one answering and it tried a command it isn't allowed to run, the request is now tried once more automatically; /jobs and the event log (telegram_lag, job_retried) show what happened.
A note I dropped in the scratch folder didn't move/sort says why: it was touched in the last 10 minutes, no rule or AI answer was sure enough (it's listed with Move buttons), notes of yours link to it by its path, it has sort: no, or the nightly limit (20) was used. /sort now runs it.
The sorter moved a note to the wrong place/sort undo puts the last pass back. Move the note yourself to where it belongs: it learns from that and won't move it again.
The phone doesn't ringIs every item on the app's checklist ticked? Battery: unrestricted, and Kurooto off any "sleeping apps" list (Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, OnePlus, Huawei). Then dashboard → Phone → Ring my phone.
Today says "Nothing here yet" on one phone, but another phone has itThat phone's key didn't reach your call line when it paired. Open the app with the internet on (0.6.1 sends it again; on 0.6.0 swipe the app away and open it again); the list comes within a minute or two while the computer is on.
Android won't install the appAllow installing unknown apps for your browser when asked; for Play Protect, "More details" → "Install anyway".
iPhone gets no notificationsOpen Kurooto from the Home Screen (not Safari), turn notifications on in it, iOS 16.4 or newer, and Settings → Notifications → Kurooto.
Questions don't show on the phoneIs question in notify.app_kinds? On Android, is the "Questions and approvals" notification category on? Is the computer connected to your call line?
"Not sent: tap to try again"The phone was offline or the call line didn't answer. Tap to retry, or answer on Telegram.
"That answer is too old (or this phone's clock is off)"Set the phone's clock to automatic, then answer again.
"That's no longer open"It was answered somewhere else or expired. Nothing to do.
"That answer couldn't be opened here. Pair the phone again."Pair the phone again (for example after kurooto rotate).
A device paired that wasn't me/phones, then /unpair <id>. If a phone was lost, run kurooto rotate.
"Waiting for your computer" under a chat message, a file or an answerYour computer is off or not connected to your call line. It goes through by itself once the computer is back; nothing to resend.
"Your call line is holding as much as it can for your computer"The phone outbox is full (100 things, 20 MB of files). Wait till the computer is back, or use Telegram.
The app's chat says "Your call line doesn't have the chat yet"Your computer runs an older Kurooto, or its call line wasn't redeployed yet. Update Kurooto; it redeploys the line by itself (or Features → Phone calls → set up again).
No answer in the app's chat, but Telegram worksAnswers to what you wrote in the app come back to the app. Is the app's notification category "Messages" on? Open the chat: it catches up from your call line.
A file in the chat says "stays on your computer"Your call line wasn't connected, or the file is over 10 MB. Turn on Drive upload to get files as a Drive link, or open it on the computer at the path shown.
A file link says "This file is gone"Copies on your call line last a day. Ask for it again (or turn on Drive upload, whose links last drive_upload.temp_days).
"This link doesn't open the file"The link was cut short (copied by hand?). Open it from the chat's button.
No Chat button on the iPhone appYour call line is older: update Kurooto on the computer; it redeploys the line. Or open <your call line>/talk.

Research: "isn't a git repository (no commits to follow)"Only a note. git init the folder to follow it, or /repos remove it.
Research add-on: "the LaTeX tracker needs Tectonic or LaTeX"There's no LaTeX program on this computer: /latex install fetches Tectonic, or install TeX Live, MiKTeX or MacTeX.
Research add-on: "LaTeX folder missing"You moved or deleted it: /latex remove <folder>, then /latex add <new folder>.
/latex add says there are several documentsName the one to follow: /latex add <folder> main.tex.
/runs compare or kurooto runlog diff says "matches N runs"Give more of the id, or use the numbers /runs shows.
A run comparison has no code diff ("isn't in <repo> any more", "the repository isn't on this computer")The commit was rebased away or the repository moved; the config and metrics are still compared.
An arXiv search is refusedAn unclosed bracket or quote in the query.
No papers digest arrivedNo new papers that week (it only says so when a source failed), or on battery it waits for the charger.
kurooto watch says "only shown on this computer"Kurooto wasn't running: kurooto start.

The dashboard won't openkurooto open, or http://127.0.0.1:8770; the port may have moved (kurooto paths → web.json). It opens only on the computer Kurooto runs on.

A setting isn't on the Settings pageA feature's settings appear once it's on. Telegram's pairing (telegram.chat_id and the like) is set by pairing, not on the page.
The Terminals tab says it's off, or a terminal won't typeFeatures → Terminals → Turn on. On Linux, a terminal from another user account is refused on purpose.

Kurooto Terminal looks old in Windows TerminalClose every Windows Terminal window and open it again.
The system palette doesn't use my coloursSettings → Look shows what Kurooto found; changes show within half a minute. Point look.theme_file at a kitty theme or a matugen or pywal colors.json.
Your add-ons: a problem in kurooto doctor/addons shows which file and the error. Fix it, then restart Kurooto (kurooto stop, kurooto start).

Your computer

What you seeWhat to do
Something only happens when I plug inThat's the battery saver. Set power.on_battery to normal to keep everything running.
The laptop still sleeps with the lid closed on the charger (Linux)Turn on awake.lid. If your system refuses the lid hold to background programs (doctor says so), allow it in polkit or set HandleLidSwitchExternalPower=ignore in logind.conf.
kurooto autostart boot: "Linux didn't allow that from here"Run loginctl enable-linger in a terminal, then kurooto autostart boot again.
kurooto ask, call, send, ask-me or ask-approval say Kurooto isn't runningkurooto on, then try again. --timeout is in minutes.
A low-disk warning/space clean removes Kurooto's own old files; free space elsewhere too.
"Kurooto didn't connect to <host> ... it isn't on the list"privacy.egress is enforce and that host isn't on Kurooto's list (an address changed in settings, an add-on). If you trust it, add it to privacy.allow_hosts in Settings (["feeds.example.org"]), or set privacy.egress back to record. /egress shows what was blocked.

Updates, backups and moving

What you seeWhat to do
kurooto update says it can't update itselfRun the install line again (see Getting started); it repairs and updates.
"Couldn't prepare Kurooto …"It tries again later.
Kurooto went back to the previous versionThe new one crashed three times in its first five minutes; you were told on Telegram. It won't offer that version again.
Backups: "the backup folder isn't there"Plug the drive in or sign in to the cloud app; the next backup comes within the hour.
Backups: "not enough room in the backup folder"Free space there, lower backups.keep, or turn backups.index off.
A restore went wrongkurooto restore the zip in restore-safety/ (in the data folder) puts things back as they were.
Restore says a file is still open in another programStop Kurooto (kurooto stop) and try again.
Restore: "That passphrase doesn't open this profile"It's the passphrase you picked for Profile sync, not your GitHub password. Nothing was changed.
Restore: "…was changed or damaged, so it can't be trusted"A file on the kurooto-profile branch was edited outside Kurooto. Revert that edit on GitHub, then restore again.
Profile sync: "another computer saved your profile since this one did"Restore it here (kurooto profile restore), or /profile save to make this computer's the one.
Kurooto asks Update / Later every few hoursupdates.mode is ask, and Later only puts it off until the next check. Tap Update or Skip this version, or set updates.mode to night.
