Commands

Everything you can type, in one place: Telegram commands, the plain-English requests Kurooto understands instantly, the kurooto terminal commands, and the dashboard's keyboard shortcuts. Each row links to the page that explains it.

  • On Telegram, typing / shows a menu of the commands of the features you have on, plus your skills. /help lists the same as an index, one line per group with its commands; /help <group> (/help calls) shows one group in full, with a line each and what you can just say, /help all every group.
  • A command of a feature that's off answers "X is off: Features → X → Turn on" and does nothing else.
  • Commands work in any case (/Remind is /remind), and /remind@yourbot works in a group-style chat too.
  • Most commands take the rest of the line as their argument; /journal followed by a new line works as well.
  • The same plain words also work in the dashboard's Today box and Ctrl+K, in kurooto ask, and many of them on a call.
  • Commands and plain words work in the phone app's chat (Android and iPhone) exactly as on Telegram (Phone app). Without Telegram, /pause, /resume, /status, /hold, /calls and /focus answer there too, and /help lists them.

Plain-English requests understood instantly

These are answered at once on your computer, without asking an AI, so they work even when the AI is out of free requests. Anything else goes to your AI (with Chat on). Long messages, or several requests in one message, always go to the AI. A request that asks a question only matches the requests below that answer questions. Times about the past ("since I didn't wake up at 12") are never used as the time of a call or reminder.

Phrasings you taught with the Wrong button are matched first, as a whole message. See Teaching Kurooto. A short message none of these take, but about equally close to two of them, gets both as buttons ("Did you mean ...?") instead of a guess (Talking to Kurooto).

Effort prefixes. Start a message for the AI with low:, medium: or high: to choose how hard it thinks. See AI brains.

Terminal commands (kurooto ...)

Open a terminal (PowerShell on Windows, Terminal on a Mac) and type these. You never need them for everyday use; they help with setup, repairs and scripts. kurooto <command> --help shows each one's options.

How this list was checked

Every Telegram command above comes from the code's command list (each feature's commands() and Kurooto's own, in core/commands.py), every plain-English row from each feature's instant requests (their examples are what /help shows), and every terminal command from kurooto --help. A test fails when a command isn't in /help.

Some of these never happen without your tap (an alarm, a call, a new mail sender, anything sent out, a repository, deleting your data): Never without your tap.
