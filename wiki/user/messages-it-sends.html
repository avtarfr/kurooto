Messages Kurooto sends by itself

Most of what Kurooto sends is an answer to something you did. The rest comes by itself: a reminder, a check-in, a digest, a warning. This page lists every kind, why it comes and how to get fewer of it. If a message surprised you, tap its Why? button, or send /why.

Calm: Why?, Less of this and the daily limit

What it does. On by default. Every message that comes by itself records why it came: which feature sent it, what for, the setting that controls it, and what it was about (the deadline, the class, the project). Then:

  • Why? (a button on Telegram) answers from that record, for example: "Why: Deadlines, a reminder before something on your deadline list is due. About: Report on groups. Setting: deadlines.ping_h = 24 (Settings → Deadlines)." It also says when it was sent, how many have come today, and whether it waited for the evening bundle and why. A message with no reason recorded came from an add-on or a script: "No reason recorded"; Kurooto never guesses.
  • Less of this offers up to three choices. Each changes one setting and is followed by an Undo button:
  • Stop: that kind stops. Where the feature has its own setting, that's what changes (practice offers: quiz.offer_at emptied; a check-in: its time cleared; after-class questions: After-class notes turned off). Otherwise the kind goes on Calm's own list, notify.calm.stopped.
  • Fewer: where it makes sense: the mail digest once a day instead of twice, the papers digest weekly, quiet projects asked about half as often, no after-class question for that one subject, at most one Classroom announcement a day (the rest wait for the evening).
  • Another time: that kind always waits for the evening bundle (notify.calm.evening).

"Done: no more doubt lists." with Undo confirms it.

The phone app, the desktop and calls get the plain message, without these buttons.

The daily limit. At most 6 messages and 1 call a day come by themselves (notify.calm.messages, notify.calm.calls). Past that:

  • the first time a day the limit holds a message, you're told at once, in one line on Telegram and in the phone app's chat: "That's today's 6 messages that came by themselves. The rest wait for the evening bundle at 20:00, which lists each one." It has three buttons:
  • Send them as they come today: the limit is off for the rest of today, and what it held so far comes now, in the order it came (with Why? and Less of this). Tomorrow the limit holds again.
  • Raise to 12: notify.calm.messages doubles (6 to 12), with Undo; what waited comes now, up to the new limit.
  • Fine: nothing changes; they come at 20:00.

It comes at most once a day, and only for the daily limit: messages held for exam season, a quiet week, Another time, Fewer or the per-kind rules below don't bring it. With notify.calm.messages = 0 it doesn't come (you chose that). It isn't counted.

  • messages wait for one evening bundle at 20:00 (notify.calm.evening_at): one Telegram message, "This evening's bundle: 4 messages that waited, held by the daily limit of 6" (or, when there were several reasons, "(3 held by the daily limit of 6; 1 because you asked for these in the evening)"), each item numbered, with each item's buttons numbered to match ("2: Nothing new"). Long bundles come in parts. Files that waited follow it.
  • a question that waits is still open meanwhile (/q, and on your next call). If it's answered before 20:00 it isn't in the bundle. Replying to the bundle answers its question when it's the only one in it. The phone app gets the waiting questions one by one at bundle time.
  • a call past the limit isn't placed: a check-in comes as a message instead; anything else waits in the bundle with a Call now link. A call that rings again (its repeat) counts once.
  • after the bundle time, messages come as they are.

Never held, never stopped, not counted: the alarm (and the alarm to leave for an exam, and "tonight's alarm might not ring"); approvals; your own reminders (reminders, calls you asked for, quiz times and study sessions you set, calendar event reminders); a deadline less than 12 hours away (and its call); anything urgent (Kurooto can't work until you act: a sign-in that ended, crashes); a newly paired device on your call line; the evening bundle itself. These have no Less of this. Answers to what you asked or did (AI answers, /sync, a skill you ran, a button you tapped) aren't counted at all.

Update notices aren't counted either ("Kurooto updated to 0.5.19."), and several in one day fold into one: on Telegram the first one is changed in place to the newest, "Kurooto updated to 0.5.19 (and 2 earlier today).", so three overnight updates leave one message. Where the first can't be changed (no Telegram, or it was deleted), the newest comes as a new message with the same "(and 2 earlier today)". They still have Why? and Less of this.

Classroom → notes sends one message per check, once the AI has filed everything that check found, whichever courses it came from: "Classroom: filed 9 new items into your notes:" and the items by title (the first eight, then "and 1 more").

Exam season. With Exam season turned on and its "Only deadlines, quizzes and the alarm by day" part on, during the exam period only Deadlines, Quiz, the alarm and Exam season itself come during the day; everything else that comes by itself waits for the evening bundle, even after 20:00 (then until the next one). The daily limit still applies to what comes. Calls from other features are held the same way.

Getting used to things (tuned while dreaming). With Dreaming on, each night Kurooto replays the messages it sent you by itself in the last 45 days under a few variants of when they come, and scores each variant with what you actually did: answered ones that would still reach you in time count for it, interruptions you ignored count against it, and holding back one you answered quickly counts heavily against it. If a variant clearly beats things as they are (dreaming.tune_margin), it makes that one change and says so in the morning message with Undo: "Learned while you slept: questions about quiet projects wait for your evening bundle once 2 in a row go unanswered; over the last 14 days that would have saved 8 ignored pings and delayed none you answered." At most one change a night. The changes it can make:

  • after N unanswered in a row (notify.calm.habituate): that kind waits for the evening bundle once you've left the last N unanswered (3 hours or older), and comes as it happens again as soon as you answer one;
  • the evening bundle for that kind (notify.calm.evening, the same as Another time);
  • only at the hours you answered it (notify.calm.windows, e.g. 10:00-13:00): at other times it waits.

It never touches what always comes (the alarm, your reminders and calls, approvals, deadlines under 12 hours), kinds that can't wait for the evening (practice offers, check-ins, doubt lists), the kinds in dreaming.never_tune (deadline reminders and calls among them), or anything you set yourself (stopped, fewer, the evening list, the limit, the night). It only moves messages that had something to answer, and it can't judge what it never sent. After an Undo it leaves that kind alone for 30 days. /dream policy shows the rules as they are and the last nights' decisions; dreaming.tune_messages = false turns it off. The same night pass also tunes the effort your requests get, which AI goes first and Start-by's sizes (Teaching Kurooto); all of them together still make at most one change a night.

Once a month, Kurooto looks at the kind of question you answer least. If 8 or more came in 30 days and you answered a fifth of them or fewer: "In the last 30 days you answered 1 of 19 after-class questions. Turn them off? (Turns off After-class notes; Undo puts it back.)" with Apply / No. Only for questions you're meant to answer (marked "monthly" in the table below), never for reminders. No keeps that kind out of it for 90 days. It needs a month of records first.

What leaves your computer: nothing. The records stay in calm.db in Kurooto's state folder for 45 days: when, the reason, the setting, what it was about, the first 300 characters (forgotten after privacy.keep_days when you set it: privacy.md), and whether you tapped or answered it; a waiting message in full until its evening.

Turn it off / undo: every Less-of-this choice and every Apply has an Undo button that puts that one setting back (a feature it turned off goes back on). /calm lists what's stopped, with a button to start each again. /calm off turns Calm off; anything already waiting still comes at the evening bundle.

Keep or drop: the features you don't use

What it does. Kurooto counts, on this computer, what you actually use of each feature: requests it answered at once ("remind me at 9 to call mum"), its commands, taps on its messages' buttons, its tools used on a call, and your replies to its messages. On day 10 (counted from Kurooto's first start, or from the update that brought this), once setup is done, one message lists every feature that's on with its use in those 10 days and the messages it sent by itself:

Ten days in: what you used of each feature that's on, and what it sent by itself. - Weekly summary: not used; 1 message by itself - Reminders: used 12 times (9 requests, 3 commands)

Under it, per feature (the least used first): Keep, Quiet and Off.

  • Keep: nothing changes, and it isn't asked about again for 90 days.
  • Quiet: the feature keeps working (its commands, requests and scheduled work), but what it sends by itself stops: its kinds of message go on Calm's stopped list. Your own reminders, the alarm and approvals still come. Calls it places follow their own settings.
  • Off: the feature is turned off, as in Features (its data stays).

Quiet and Off each come with an Undo button. Then monthly: a feature that's on, hasn't been used for 60 days and still runs something on a schedule gets the same question. Nothing is turned down without a tap. Telegram, which these come on, is never asked about.

CommandDoes
/keepeach feature that's on, what you used of it in the last 30 days, and what it sent by itself
/keep 60the same for 60 days (up to 90)
/keep weeklyone feature, with its Keep / Quiet / Off buttons

Settings: keep.ask (true): false stops the questions; /keep still shows the counts.

What leaves your computer: nothing. The counts are in keep.json in Kurooto's state folder: per feature and day, only numbers (kept 90 days), and when each feature was last used.

Turn it off / undo: the Undo button under Quiet or Off puts it back; keep.ask = false, or Less of this → Stop on the question, stops the questions.

Going offline and back online

What it does. Kurooto tells you on Telegram when it goes away and when it's back. On by default; nothing ever rings for this.

  • A clean stop (kurooto stop or off, the dashboard, signing out, shutting down): just before it goes, "Kurooto is going offline (shutting down): calls and reminders will wait until it's back". With Phone calls set up: "reminders will wait until it's back; your call line still places scheduled calls". It never holds up a shutdown by more than a few seconds. An update's restart says nothing.
  • Sleep, a crash, a lost network or a power cut: the computer can't speak then. With Phone calls set up, your call line notices the link gone for 10 minutes and says it: "Kurooto has been offline since 22:10 (asleep, off or without internet): ..." on the phone app, and on Telegram if you allowed it (below). Without Phone calls there's no message for these, only the "back" one afterwards.
  • Back: when Kurooto starts, when the computer wakes, and when the network works again: "Kurooto is back online (offline since 22:10, 7 h)" or "Back online: the network was down from 14:02 to 14:31 (29 min)", with what it caught up on (reminders that went out late, calls your call line placed meanwhile).
  • The network: when Telegram or the call line can't be reached several times in a row, the network counts as down from the first failure. Messages meanwhile are kept and follow the "back online" message, in order, each marked "Delayed, from 14:05".

Not every blip. Nothing for less than 10 minutes away (except right after a goodbye). Several sleeps or drops close together give one message, once the computer has been up for a minute. At night (your night hours, or bedtime to the alarm) and in focus time it waits for the morning; if nothing told you Kurooto had gone and it's back before then, nothing is said.

Telegram from your call line. For the line to tell you on Telegram while your computer can't, it needs your bot's token. Kurooto asks once (Approve / Deny). Approve: the token and your chat id go to your call line on your own Cloudflare account. Deny: only the phone app hears from the line (add "app" to notify.presence.channels).

Kurooto keeps itself working

What it does. Every feature that's on has one health check, and Kurooto runs all of them once a day (21:00, before bedtime, so tonight's alarm is checked in time). It stays quiet unless something is wrong.

What's checked (only features that are on). Only hours the computer was awake and Kurooto wasn't paused count, and for work that waits for the charger, battery time doesn't count either: a shut lid never makes a sync "late".

When you hear about it:

  • tonight's alarm might not ring: at once ("Set your phone's own alarm too");
  • your Telegram bot stopped answering: at once, on the desktop and the phone app (Telegram can't carry it);
  • a problem that could make you miss a deadline or exam in the next 48 hours (Classroom, Calendar, the call line): one message now, with a Fix button when there's one;
  • anything else waits for the weekly summary ("Needs a look: ..."); with the weekly summary off, one message on Sundays.

Each problem is told once; if it's fixed and breaks again, you hear again.

Fixes. Before telling you, Kurooto tries a fix that's safe to run unasked, then looks again ten minutes later. There are five, all limited to Kurooto's own state, never your notes:

A sign-in that ended has no fix Kurooto can do; the message says what to do. After you tap Fix, you hear ten minutes later whether it worked.

Where to look: /health (what's wrong, with Fix buttons; "Everything Kurooto checks is working (12 checks)" when all is well); /health now also asks online services; the dashboard's "needs a look" badge and the Features tab; kurooto doctor (kurooto doctor --fix <id> runs a fix); the weekly summary.

Lighter days and when you've gone quiet

Lighter days (notify.calm.lighter_after, on). The day after an exam (a quiz or exam on your deadline list whose time passed the day before) or a heavy day (8 hours or more on the Study tracker), Kurooto goes easier: half the daily limit of messages that come by themselves (3 instead of 6; the rest wait for the evening bundle), and your first check-in an hour later. The morning message says so in one line ("A lighter day after your exam (Linear Algebra midsem): fewer messages from me and the first check-in an hour later."), with Not today, which puts the day back as usual. Without Dreaming, the line comes on its own once your night hours are over.

When you've gone quiet (availability.absence_days, 2; 0 turns it off). If nothing came from you for two days (no message, no button, no answered call) while Kurooto kept running, one message: "Haven't heard from you in two days. All good? Nothing's urgent.", with I'm fine and Busy, quiet for a week. It's never repeated, never followed up and never a call: the next one can only come after you've been back. It waits out the night, focus, /hold, /pause and a quiet week. Busy, quiet for a week: no calls for a week (like /hold; /calls lifts it), and whatever comes by itself waits for one message each evening until then; the alarm, approvals and your own reminders still come. Undo under the reply ends it early.

Where messages show up

Every message goes to Telegram. Some also go elsewhere:

SettingDefaultEffect
notify.app_kinds["call", "question", "approval"]what also reaches your paired phone app. Add "reminder", "deadline", "result" or "info"; remove "question" and "approval" to keep them on Telegram only
notify.desktoptruepop-up notifications on this computer
notify.desktop_kinds["call", "question", "approval", "deadline", "update"]which kinds pop up on this computer (anything pops up there when no other channel took it)
telegram.silent_kinds["call"]kinds sent to Telegram without a sound (while the phone app is paired, since it rings itself)

When you answer a question or tap a button somewhere, the copies elsewhere (the phone app's notification) are taken down.
