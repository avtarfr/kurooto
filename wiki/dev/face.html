Face presence (modules/face/, 0.8.10)

Kurooto recognises its owner's face with OpenCV's YuNet (detection) and SFace (recognition), on the CPU, on this computer only, and greets him by voice when he sits down after an absence, then opens Voice's conversation (Portal.invite). Off by default (a module); nothing watches until he enrols on the dashboard's Face page. User page: ../user/face.md. Design: build-specs/0.8.10/face.md (outside the repo).

The rule above all others: a face match never authorises anything. It isn't one of core/tap.py:WAYS, never answers an approval or unlocks anything. tests/unit/test_face.py:test_a_face_never_authorises_anything checks that core/tap.py, core/ask.py and core/tools.py never read the face module. One narrow exception, its own switch (0.8.13, voice.speaker_id.face_assist, on): a voice Voice ID already scored inside its "not sure" margin counts as the owner while the watcher's last frame (at most core/nearby.py:VIEW_S old) shows exactly one face and it's his (the watcher's view emit -> nearby.set_view). A face alone, a voice below the margin, two voices or strict strictness never do; see voice.md.

The pipeline

Camera.read()  (BGR, 640x480 asked; one grab() first; CAP_PROP_BUFFERSIZE 1)
  Frames.mean(frame) < 8 for 10 frames -> "camera covered"
  Detector.detect(frame, 0.6)   YuNet on a 320-wide copy; boxes and 5 landmarks scaled back; NMS 0.3
  for each face, score >= 0.8 (0.9 enrolling) and height >= face.recognise.min_face_px:
      Embedder.align(frame, face)   FaceRecognizerSF.alignCrop -> 112x112
      Embedder.stats(crop)          grey: variance of the Laplacian, mean, share >= 250
          lenient (watching): blur >= BLUR_MIN / 2, 30 <= mean <= 225, else ignored
      Embedder.feature(crop)        128 floats, L2-normalised, a Python list
      Template.classify(e)          owner (>= T_hi) | unsure (>= T_lo) | stranger
  del frame, crops; a stranger's or unsure face's numbers are dropped at once

Backend and target are set explicitly (DNN_BACKEND_OPENCV, DNN_TARGET_CPU). The watcher uses 1 thread (cv2.setNumThreads(1)), enrolment 4. Measured on the owner's CPU (spec §2, synthetic input): YuNet at 320x240 about 9 ms on 1 thread, SFace about 30 ms per face: under 5 % of a core at 1 frame a second.

The maths is pure Python on lists (core/biometric.py:dot, mean_unit): numpy isn't in Kurooto's own install, and the sizes (≤ 3 centroids × 5,000 impostors × 128) take well under a second.

The watcher (watch.py)

States: off, paused(why), resting (camera closed, input is presence), watching (camera open), away.

tick() (every 0.25 s from the thread face-watch; Face.background only keeps the thread alive and stops it):

  1. A clock jump of more than 30 s (a sleep) or an unlock opens a 10 s "woke" window. Locked: idle isn't read.
  2. Input (idle < 2 s, unlocked) is presence: _input(); a gap since present_at of ≥ greet.after_absent_min starts a *return* (returning = {since, gap}), which waits 30 s for his face. Input ends away ("back").
  3. Away (away.on): no presence for away.after_min while unlocked and watching, or locked that long → "left".
  4. paused() (the module's pause_reason()) or a camera problem: close, state paused.
  5. The camera is wanted when idle ≥ watch.idle_s, or locked with watch.when_locked on, or (woke, confirming a return, or already open while idle can be read) within 6 s of input becoming active, or an add-on holds it (held(), below; step 4 has already passed). Otherwise it closes (resting).
  6. A frame is due at the rate: watch.fps (fps_battery on battery), 2 fps for 5 s after the first owner frame, divided by slow (doubled, up to 8, whenever a frame takes more than 150 ms), never below 0.2.
  7. Arrival: a return is pending, recognise.frames (3) owner frames among the last 4 within 6 s, and not *still* (every pair of those embeddings with cosine > 0.995 and landmarks moved < 0.5 px: a propped-up photo; it waits). Emits arrived {away_min, others}; others = a second face (score ≥ 0.6) in any of those frames.

Camera problems back off: busy 60 s (Linux /proc/*/fd holders, Windows' CapabilityAccessManager record), no camera 60 s, blocked 300 s, covered 300 s. Camera.open() raises CameraError("busy" | "no camera" | "blocked"); on Linux it tries only free() cameras, so "busy" means every candidate is held. A face.camera.* change swaps the watcher's Camera on the next tick (Face._camera_changed); before 0.8.13 it took a restart.

Pause reasons, in order (watch.py:PAUSES, Face.pause_checks() evaluates them lazily in that order): feature off · not enrolled (or "re-enrol needed (the recognition model changed)") · not set up yet · paused by you · Kurooto is paused · on battery (pause mode) · screen locked · you're on a call · focus · night hours · enrolling · camera in use by another program · no camera · camera blocked by the system · camera covered · template locked.

Decision worth knowing: when the computer can't say how long it's been idle (IdleProbe.idle_seconds() is None), the camera opens only after an unlock or a wake (or to confirm a return), never "while idle", and "already open" doesn't keep it open: it closes when that window ends. Failing closed keeps the camera free (0.8.10 review: before the fix it stayed open for good once a wake had opened it).

Hooks for add-ons (0.8.12)

Face presence doesn't watch all the time: the camera opens only after watch.idle_s without input, for 10 s after an unlock or a wake, and while it confirms an arrival; while you type it closes after the 6 s grace. An add-on that needs the room in view holds it. All on Face (app.modules["face"]):

Close and facing, for Voice (0.8.13)

voice.listen.need_face (Voice's switch; voice.md). The watcher's view emit gained sizes: for each face at SCORE_OTHER or more, (height_px, yaw) (watch._yaw: vision.pose's yaw, None when the landmarks can't give one). Face._on_watch("view") calls nearby.set_facing(app, faces, close_facing(sizes)) and drops the list. close_facing counts faces with height_px >= voice.listen.face_near * owner_desk_height()["px"] (no desk height: face.recognise.min_face_px) and vision.yaw_degrees(yaw) <= voice.listen.face_yaw_deg; yaw_degrees is atan(|yaw| / YAW_K) with YAW_K = 0.3 / tan(25 deg), the scale Pose.yaw documents (an estimate from five landmarks). core/nearby.py keeps (time, faces, facing) per frame for FACING_KEEP_S (60 s, at most 600 rows) and the last sighting time: times and counts only, never boxes, pictures, numbers or identity. A covered frame emits no view, so it counts as the camera not looking.

0.8.17 (voice.listen.look_to_talk): view also carries owner, the best owner face's (height_px, yaw, pitch) (watch._pitch), or None. Numbers for where a face points only, dropped with the frame. By the eyes (0.8.17, voice.listen.look_gaze, gaze.py)

  • The part. parts.py: iris = iris_landmark.tflite (MediaPipe Iris, Apache-2.0, 2,640,568 bytes), from storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-assets/iris_landmark.tflite?generation=1662065468653224, sha256 d1744d2a09c25f501d39eba4faff47e53ecca8852c5ce19bce8eeac39357521f (the same bytes as google/mediapipe v0.8.10's mediapipe/modules/iris_landmark/). Optional: EYES = ["model:iris"], not in WANTS; fetched by Face.gaze_setup() (POST face/gaze/setup, the page's Get the eye model); install_from copies it when the folder has it. Loaded with cv2.dnn.readNetFromTFLite (checked with opencv-python-headless 4.14: NCHW blob, RGB 0..1, outputs output_eyes_contours_and_brows (1, 213) and output_iris (1, 15)).
  • Reading. CvGazer.eyes(frame, face): for each of YuNet's eye points, a square EYE_CROP (1.0) x the eyes' distance, turned level by the roll, cut from the full frame at its own resolution (warpAffine, inverse map), then shrunk to 64x64 (INTER_AREA); the picture's left eye (landmark 0) mirrored, as MediaPipe's right-eye path does, and its points mirrored back. offset(contour, iris): corners are contour points 0 and 8, lids' middles 4 and 12; h = 2t - 1 along the corner line (picture left -1 .. right 1), v across it (down positive) in eye widths, open = |p4 - p12| / width; eyes_of averages the eyes with open >= OPEN_MIN (0.12), None for none.
  • Angles. angles(h, v, yaw, pitch, cal): atan(yaw / YAW_K) - atan(cal.yaw / YAW_K) minus asin(h / EYE_H) - asin(cal.h / EYE_H) (the picture isn't mirrored, pose's yaw is), and atan((pitch - cal.pitch) / PITCH_K) plus asin(v / EYE_V) - asin(cal.v / EYE_V); EYE_H 0.8 and EYE_V 0.4 from an eyeball radius of about 12 mm over a 30 mm eye. Estimates; calibration sets the zero, the tolerances absorb the gain.
  • Calibration. Session kind gaze (enrol.py, Face.gaze_start, POST face/gaze/start, guarded camera.open): per frame, one sure face, alone, |yaw| <= CAL_YAW (0.2), classified owner (SFace), eyes read -> Calibration.feed; done after CAL_S (3 s) and CAL_MIN (8) readings; result() refuses a median absolute deviation of h over CAL_SPREAD (0.2) or too few frames (gives up CAL_MAX_S, 15 s, after the first). The page gets {kind: "gaze", ok, reason, share} and {kind: "gaze_result", ok, message}, never numbers. Face._gaze_done stores {h, v, yaw, pitch, n, at} as Template.gaze (sealed; face.json gets only gaze_at); gaze_clear() (POST face/gaze/clear) empties it; forget drops it with the template. Event face_gaze {calibrated}.
  • Deciding. The watcher reads eyes only while gaze() (Face._gaze_now) hands a gazer: look_to_talk on, Voice's own hold on the camera (_look_listening), gaze_calibration() (look_gaze on, the part, a valid calibration). The "view" emit gains eyes=(h, v) (or None) and frame=(w, h). Face.looking(owner, eyes, frame) with a calibration: close as before, head within face_yaw_deg, then |angles| <= look_gaze_yaw_deg / look_gaze_pitch_deg (10, 10); no eyes (a blink) keeps the last verdict for BLINK_HOLD_S (1 s). Without one: the head rule above. gaze_status(): {on, model, calibrated, at, using: eyes | head, why, now: {looking, yaw_deg, pitch_deg} (5 s fresh), mb}, in status()["gaze"] and Voice's status()["look_to_talk"]["gaze"].
  • Rate and cost. face.watch.look_fps (5, at most watch.LOOK_MAX_FPS 5, halved by slow like fps) applies while _look_listening() (_watch_cfg()["look_fps"], else 0). Measured on a Ryzen 7 7435HS, one thread, per full frame at 1080x1440: YuNet on the 320-wide copy 13.7 ms, frames.mean 4.0, SFace align + feature 28.7, two eyes through the iris model 6.7: about 53 ms (47 ms at 480x640), so 5 fps is about a quarter of one core while Voice listens.

Camera resolution (0.8.17)

CvDetector already ran YuNet on a 320-wide copy (now INTER_AREA, no aliasing from 1080 wide) and scaled the points back; SFace's alignCrop and the eyes use the full frame. Every size-dependent rule is now relative: vision.px_scale(w, h) = shorter side / REF_PX (480) scales face.recognise.min_face_px (watcher and enrolment), the still-picture check (STILL_PX), enrolment's far step (min_face_px + 20) and repeat check (REPEAT_PX); the enrolment baseline gains share (front face height / frame height), used for the near and far steps when known; owner_desk_height() from the enrolment uses share (measured: True; an older one's px / face.camera.height is a guess and is compared in pixels as before); close_facing(sizes, frame) compares h_px with desk.h * frame height * face_near when the desk height is measured, else pixels as before; with no desk height, min_face_px * px_scale. _shown, _person, "seen" and tracking were shares already. Tests run both 480x640 and 1080x1440.

Tracking every face (0.8.12)

So the being's eye (and an add-on's) can follow anyone in view, not just the owner. Switch face.track.on (on); rate face.track.fps (10, at most 15). Tracking never opens the camera: it only works while the watcher's rules have it open (idle, a wake, a confirmation, an add-on's hold).

Face._track_mode() → Watcher.tracking (read once per full tick):

ModeWhenWhat runs
TRACK_OFFface.track.on off, or nobody wants facesnothing: no tracks, no list (as 0.8.11)
TRACK_SLOWan add-on holds the camera (any reason) or follows faces (watch_faces), no page in sighttracks updated on the recognition frames only (watch.fps)
TRACK_FASTa dashboard page in sight (Live.watched()), or a hold whose reason contains "track"detection-only frames (Watcher.track_tick: Camera.read + YuNet, never SFace) between the recognition frames, at track.fps

The thread (Face._run): a full tick() (pause reasons, input, the camera's rules, recognition when due) every TICK_S = 0.25 s; between, track_tick() only; it sleeps Watcher.wait_s(): until the next detection frame or full tick, never under WAIT_MIN_S = 20 ms. A detection-only frame over 60 ms halves the tracking rate (to 1/8 at most); 30 quick ones in a row undo one halving.

Tracks (Watcher._update_tracks, positions in memory only, cleared when the camera closes): each frame's faces (score ≥ 0.6, any size) are matched to the tracks greedily, nearest centre first, within TRACK_MATCH = 1 face height; unmatched faces get a new id (counting up from 1 per watcher); a track missing TRACK_MISS = 3 frames in a row is gone. The owner flag comes from recognition frames: the face recognised as you sets it (on that track only), a stranger clears it, unsure and unjudged faces keep it.

Micro-movement m (Watcher._move): against the track as it was MICRO_WINDOW_S = 0.4 s ago (the last frame when frames are further apart): centre shift + size change + the largest landmark shift relative to the face's centre (a nod or a turn moves the nose, a wide mouth movement its corners), in face heights, minus MICRO_JITTER = 0.03, per second, over MICRO_FULL = 0.3, clipped to 0..1; it rises 60 % of the way to a new reading at once and fades with MICRO_TAU = 0.5 s. The jitter and scale are estimates from YuNet's landmark precision at 320 px wide, not measured on a camera yet: a still face may read 0.05-0.15 on a noisy webcam. YuNet's five landmarks have no lips, so talking shows only as far as it moves the mouth's corners or the head.

The message, to every open page (Face._on_watch("faces"), at most one per FACES_EVERY_S = 0.09 s, an empty list always; only while Live.watched()) and to watch_faces subscribers (every one):

{"t": "face", "kind": "faces", "faces": [{"id": 3, "x": -0.21, "y": -0.05, "h": 0.31, "owner": true, "m": 0.18}]}

x, y the box's centre in -1..1 of the camera's own picture (not mirrored, as face_seen), h its height's share of the frame. Never an event, never kept. face_seen is unchanged (owner only, recognition frames, ≤ 5 a second).

Time at the desk (0.9.0, L22, face.desk_time, off): Face.desk_spans(lo, hi) pairs face_arrived / face_back with face_left from the event log (a stretch open at lo starts there, one still going ends now); desk_line(lo, hi, when) says the total and the longest (under 15 min: ""); weekly_lines() gives the week's for the weekly summary, and checkins._desk_line(day) the check-in day's for the evening message and call (a step that says it once, never as a judgement). The break bid at Brain's door (L22's third part) waits for L18 (Face -> mind/breaks).

The being's eye (static/being.js, ATT/attend()): with faces fresh (< 2 s) it looks at one face and moves to another (picked at random, weighted 0.4 + m) every 3-8 s, after 0.7 s to someone new, after 1 s to a face whose m is over 0.3 and 0.25 above the current one's; a switch is a saccade (fast gaze for 140 ms). One face is followed closely (gaze rate 9 against 5), with a quick small saccade, landing a little ahead, when its m jumps by 0.12 or passes 0.35. The owner has no priority, except (0.9.0, L136, face.track.speaker: Face._on_watch adds speaker: true to the pages' faces message) while someone talks: voice() keeps ATT.talk from voice_text (who: "you", speaker = Voice ID's decision), voice_heard and voice_level above 0.2, and attend() turns to the owner's face when the words were the owner's, else the face with the highest m, within 1.5 s of speech (400 ms dwell). Booleans and the verdict word only (R2). STILL (Remove animations) or the system's reduced motion: no saccades, a slow smooth gaze (rate 1.5). Without faces (tracking off, an older daemon) it follows face_seen as before. The phone's Views → Being (being-phone.js) runs the same code but gets no face messages.

Cost, honestly: YuNet at 320x240 measured about 9 ms on one thread (above), plus Camera.read and the resize (a few ms), so TRACK_FAST at 10 a second is roughly 10-15 % of one core while the camera is open and a dashboard is in sight; that's an estimate from the 0.8.10 measurement, not measured with a camera in this release. test_the_watch_thread_sleeps_between_frames_while_tracking runs the real loop with free fakes for 1.2 s: about 12 detections and well under 0.3 s of the thread's CPU, so the loop itself doesn't spin. Lower face.track.fps or turn face.track.on off to save it.

Tests: tests/unit/test_face.py section 14 (ids across frames and list order, micro-movement still/nod/large/fade, rates and passes, the switch, the slow machine, who wants faces, the page rate limit and add-on copies, the real loop).

Enrolment and the test (enrol.py)

POST /api/face/enrol/start {cid, mode: new|add} → Face._start: refused when the feature is off, parts are missing, he's on a call, another session is open, or the camera is busy. The session's thread waits up to 3 s for the watcher to release the camera, then opens it. Session.step() at 5 fps: read, JPEG for the preview (320x240, quality 60, in memory), detect, Enrolment.judge(), push {t: "face", kind: "enrol", session, ok, reason, step, box, landmarks, quality: {blur, light, yaw, pitch, roll}, ...progress} to the page's cid only (box and landmarks as shares of the frame; numbers rounded).

judge() order (first failure is the reason): covered (10 dark frames) · no face (≥ 0.9) · two faces (> 1 at ≥ 0.6) · too far (< min_face_px) · too close (> 0.7 of the frame) · out of view (edge(): the box touching an edge, named as on the mirrored page; in the near step only the landmarks plus 8 % of the box must be inside) · tilted (|roll| > 25°) · the step's rule (near: height ≥ near_target() = min(1.25 × front median, front + 60 % of the way to 0.7 of the frame), with how far you are in the nudge) · blur (< BLUR_MIN 60) · dark (< 50; < 30 in the light step) · bright (> 210, or > 8 % blown) · the light step's difference (≥ 25 from the front step's median) · spacing (0.5 s, silent) · repeat (cosine > 0.97 and moved < 3 px) · not you (≥ 5 samples and cosine to their centroid < 0.25).

Pose (vision.py:pose): yaw = (nose_x − eyes_mid_x) / eye_distance, negated for the mirrored preview (his left is negative); roll = atan2(eye_dy, eye_dx); pitch = (nose_y − eyes_mid_y) / (mouth_mid_y − eyes_mid_y), judged against his own front-step median (Enrolment.baseline, kept in the template for Add samples).

Timeouts: no preview fetched for 15 s → paused (camera closed; Resume reopens it); 10 minutes of capturing → paused; 30 minutes without training → ended, samples dropped. train copies the samples into the template, retrains, seals, then Session.end() clears the in-memory list.

The test (kind: "test", 60 s): each face's score and class per frame; mark("me" | "other") looks 10 s and keeps only {frames, recognised, decided} or {frames, best, any_accepted} in Template.last_test.

Calibration (model.py, core/biometric.py:calibrate)

  • Groups: base (front to far), glasses, light; a group under 5 samples merges into base; centroid = normalised mean; score(x) = max_g cos(x, c_g). Learned samples join their nearest group at weight 0.5.
  • G: 5-fold held-out scores, stratified by step (folds(): each step's samples go round the folds). Enrolled samples only.
  • J = score(I) for the impostor matrix I against the full template.
  • T_far = max(quantile(J, 1 − far), mean(J) + z·std(J)), T_hi = max(0.363, T_far), T_lo = max(0.20, T_hi − 0.10). far, z from face.recognise.strictness: loose 1e-2 / 2.33, normal 1e-3 / 3.09, strict 1e-4 / 3.72. face.recognise.threshold > 0 overrides T_hi (method: "override"), never below 0.363 (a setting can make it stricter, never looser than the floor); fewer than 20 impostors or none: T_hi = 0.413 (method: "floor").
  • TAR = share of G ≥ T_hi, overall and per step. Grade: good (TAR ≥ 0.95, median(G) − T_hi ≥ 0.15, near/far/glasses/ light complete), fair (TAR ≥ 0.80), weak. Advice per step with TAR < 0.7, when median(J) > 0.25, for missing steps, and when calibrated without impostors.
  • Why no classifier or fine-tune: one identity, ~50 samples; a classifier would learn the synthetic impostors' domain gap; a fine-tune needs torch and licensable real impostor images. train()'s report holds what a later comparison needs.

The impostor set

src/kurooto/data/face-impostors.npz (fp16 emb M×128, model_sha256, source), read by Face.impostors() through load_impostors(), which refuses a set made with another SFace file. Not shipped in 0.8.10: the candidate source is SFHQ (synthetic faces, MIT repository) but its images were made through StyleGAN2, whose own licence is non-commercial, and the download needs a Kaggle account, so it waits for the owner's OK. tools/face_impostors.py builds it: YuNet ≥ 0.9 with exactly one face per image, align, embed, up to 5,000, and prints the score spread against a random half's centroid. Synthetic faces have no real lookalikes, which is why the LFW floor (0.363) stays a hard lower bound.

Learning

Watcher keeps a candidate from an arrival's frames when the frame is enrolment-grade (blur ≥ 60, 50 ≤ mean ≤ 210, ≤ 8 % blown), alone in view, and Template.learnable(e, margin) (≥ T_hi + learn.margin, cosine < 0.97 to every stored sample). Input within 60 s of the arrival emits learn; Face.learn checks learn.on and learn.every_h, appends to the FIFO pool (learn.max), retrains (thresholds still from enrolled held-outs) and emits face_learned {count}. Undo the last / Forget what it learned recompute.

A change of the SFace file (another sha256 than face.json's model_sha) means "re-enrol needed": embeddings from two models are never mixed.

Add this look (0.9.0, face.looks.on, on)

Why: a look the template doesn't know (hair up) scores between T_lo and T_hi ("unsure"); learning needs >= T_hi + margin, so it can never pick up a new look by itself, and lowering strictness would let others in too.

  • Start (camera.open, now ("typed", "button")): the Face page's Add this look (POST face/look/start {cid, label}, via page), /face add-look [label] (Face._cmd; via chat, or terminal when Incoming.via is terminal), kurooto face add-look [label] (cli.py:face_add_look: /face add-look through /api/say, then POST face/look/status every second: {session: {state, got, need} | None, last: {at, ok, message}}, counts and words only). Typed only: a spoken one (via == "voice" or a voice note) is refused, as /voice enrol. No intent and no AI tool reach it. Face.look_start refuses, in order: the switch off, _refuse() (feature off, parts, a call, another session), not enrolled or re-enrol needed, the template locked, LOOKS_MAX (6) looks. The label: printable characters, whitespace squeezed, LABEL_MAX (40), default "Look N".
  • Capture (Session(kind="look"), plan enrol.Look): one step, LOOK_TARGET (10) samples, judge() as enrolment (covered, no face, two faces, too far/close, edge, tilt, blur, dark, bright, spacing 0.5 s, repeat), with the step rule |yaw| <= LOOK_YAW (0.3) and _unlike = the frame's score against the enrolment's centroids (looks left out) >= T_lo ("That doesn't look enough like you"). The session ends by itself at the target or after LOOK_S (20) s of capturing; LOOK_WALL_S (90) s whatever happens; no page-gone pause; preview only when started from the page (a chat's look makes no JPEG). keep() (Face._look_keep) ends it at once when the switch goes off. At its end the camera closes first, then on_look(samples, ""); any other end (Stop, off, forget, the camera didn't open, time up) calls on_look([], why) once. Then {t: "face", kind: "look_result", ok, message} and the session's end. Live: {kind: "look", ok, reason, box, landmarks, quality, step, label, got, need, minimum, ready, new}.
  • The check (model.look_check(samples, learned, new, impostors, strictness, override), under _tpl_lock): fewer than LOOK_MIN (7; a held-out fold still leaves MIN_GROUP of the look) → refused; the look's mean must score >= max(FLOOR, T_lo) against the enrolment's own centroids (never another look's: no chain of looks walking away from you); training with the look must not lower T_hi; with the impostor set, the non-look held-out TAR at the new T_hi must not fall more than LOOK_TAR_DROP (0.05) below the old TAR at the old T_hi. Why both: without impostors (today, the set isn't shipped) T_hi is the floor whatever you add, so a new centroid widens the accepted region with nothing pushing back; the anchor is what keeps a far look (or someone else) out.
  • Adding: _previous = the payload (Undo the last training), Template.add_look(label, samples, at): the samples appended with step look:<8 hex>, looks += {id, label, at, n}; model.group_of gives each look its own group (>= LOOK_MIN samples, so never merged into base), then _retrain (held-out folds stratified by step, as enrolment). train()'s per_step and Template.status()'s per_step leave look steps out; face.json gets looks (a count, never a label). Learned samples may join a look's group (nearest centroid) as before.
  • Off: Template.use_looks (never stored) is set from the switch by Face._tpl_live() (_classify, _learnable, learn): off, score() skips look: centroids with the same T_hi, so it's only ever stricter.
  • Remove: POST face/look/remove {id} → Template.remove_look + _retrain, _previous set first.
  • Events: face_look {result: added | refused | too few | stopped | removed, count} (count = looks kept).
  • Messages: a chat's look ends with app.notify(..., why="face.look") (core/calm.py, exempt="own", no Stop: the answer to what you started). The page and the terminal show it themselves.

From a Voice ID turn (0.9.0, face.learn.from_voice, off, under learn.on): Seen gained good (the frame's quality, as the arrival candidate uses). Voice calls _face_learn() for an addressed turn whose verdict is owner and not assisted (a face-assisted verdict would have the face confirm itself); Face.learn_from_voice() needs nearby.view() == (1, True), no nearby.others, and Watcher.voice_candidate(): the newest owner frame within VOICE_LEARN_S (3 s), alone, good and learnable; then learn(e) with its spacing, cap, retrain and Undo. The mirror of voiceid.learn_face.

The camera per OS

See camera.py's docstring: V4L2 with IR names skipped and /proc/*/fd holders on Linux; MSMF then DSHOW, and the CapabilityAccessManager record for "in use", on Windows; AVFoundation on macOS, which shares the camera and can't say it's busy. Open-but-no-frames for 2 s means blocked (a permission) or a metadata node. kurooto face test-camera is the owner's check (3 s, numbers only).

Not verified

  • No real camera, model file or face was used: everything ran with fakes. cv2 isn't in the dev environment, so vision.py's OpenCV classes and camera.py's open() have never run here.
  • BLUR_MIN (60), the brightness limits and the pose ranges are first guesses; the first real run should tune them.
  • The expected scores (same person about 0.55 to 0.8, others 0 to 0.3, T_hi about 0.40 to 0.48) are from the literature, not measured.
  • The Windows busy check and macOS permission behaviour are written from documentation, untested.
  • (0.9.0) Add this look ran only on made-up vectors. LOOK_YAW (0.3), LOOK_S (20), LOOK_TARGET (10), the anchor max(0.363, T_lo) and LOOK_TAR_DROP (0.05) are first guesses: a real "hair up" look may sit further from the enrolment than the anchor allows, which shows as a refusal ("too far"), never as a looser threshold.
  • The SFace training-data line in the user page is from the SFace paper as remembered, not re-checked against it.
  • (0.8.17) The iris model ran here only on drawn eyes (OpenCV 4.14): it follows a drawn iris at 480x640 and 1080x1440 and with the head tilted, both eyes alike. Not tried on a real face: the mirroring of the picture's left eye, the gains EYE_H/EYE_V, the 10-degree tolerances, OPEN_MIN, glasses, dim light and the phone camera's real frames all need the owner's first real run (the Face page shows the last look's angles while Voice listens).

The owner's first real run

  1. A scratch KUROOTO_HOME and a free port: kurooto face test-camera; check the size, fps and the light coming on.
  2. Features → Face presence → Set up (about 0.18 GB); /egress should show only media.githubusercontent.com and the uv hand-off.
  3. Enrol; read the report (TAR, T_hi, grade, advice). Tune BLUR_MIN and the brightness limits if good frames are rejected.
  4. Test as yourself and as someone else (a stranger's photo on a phone); note the scores.
  5. Lock the screen, step away 20 minutes (or face.greet.after_absent_min 1), come back and unlock: the greeting should come within about 3 s; answer "what's next?" without the name.
  6. Start a video call right after sitting down: it should get the camera (Linux).
  7. /face forget: <state>/face/ is gone and face.key isn't in the keychain.
