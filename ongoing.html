In progress

What's being built for the next version, how to try versions before everyone else, and what people have asked for. Released versions are in the changelog.

Stable and beta

Right now: stable: 0.2.1 · beta: 0.5.11.

Stable (the default) gets a version once it has run on the beta channel for a while. Beta gets every version first, the day it's signed: newer features sooner, and now and then a rough edge. Both update by themselves at night, and an update that keeps crashing is rolled back on its own.

To join beta: in the dashboard, Settings → updates → channel: beta (or "updates": {"channel": "beta"} in settings.json), then kurooto update --now to get the newest at once. To leave: set it back to stable; Kurooto never goes back to an older version, so you stay where you are until stable catches up.

Always

Standing plans, never struck through.

  • The site keeps up with every release: the landing page, changelog, both wikis, the feature list and screenshots are updated with each version, so what the site says is what Kurooto does.
  • Kurooto knows itself: every feature, command and setting is in /help and the wikis the day it ships.

Open

  • Common features directly usable from the phone app (and the rest of the dashboard plan: check-in and quiz cards): the dashboard's Today page (0.5.7) and Ctrl+K (0.5.8) are done; the app's Today screen, local alarm and notification buttons are planned for 0.6.0.
  • A /help that lists everything on Telegram and in the app's chat: every command and request it understands, in groups, with a link to the user wiki for the rest.
  • Dreaming (0.5.9 has the morning message of the night's work and the loose ends; still to come: consolidating memory and spotting contradictions across notes): while you sleep and the laptop is idle, Kurooto goes over the day (chats, calls, AI requests, notes, your journal), consolidates what it learned into memory and your profile, spots contradictions and loose ends, and tells you in the morning what it tidied.
  • Dream RSI (self-improvement while dreaming) (0.5.9 has the first part: each morning it replays your test cases and names any that went wrong since yesterday; 0.5.10 learns a phrasing you twice said again in other words, gated by the same replay): the same night pass also looks at what went wrong (failed or retried requests, your corrections, call lessons, answers that claimed what they didn't do), changes its own prompts, skills and quiet settings to fix it, and keeps a change only if replaying the past cases goes better; each change is listed with Undo. It improves the way it improves: the checks themselves are revised the same way.
  • Set up from what it sees, not from settings (0.5.9 has projects with their supervisor, meetings, classes, commitments like the gym or a shift, a mail deadline's course and subjects' short names): one pass over chat and the journal (rules first, the AI only on a hit) creates projects with whoever they're with ("got a project under Prof X": the project, its supervisor, and meetings from then on), classes from "every Monday at 9 I have a lab", routines, a deadline's course, subject aliases; one line to say so, and /inferred to undo. Alarms, calls and anything outward still need a tap.
  • Preview the organic blobs in the phone app, as in the dashboard.
  • A better-looking phone app (Material design): Material 3 look and components (colour from the wallpaper on Android 12+, proper app bar, cards, bottom navigation), still one small APK that works on Android 6.
  • Make the UI more intuitive, and keep improving the setup wizard's elements.
  • Wiki-level documentation: a user wiki and a developer wiki (for people and for AI agents), each a page on the site, covering every nook and cranny; one full read equips someone to use, develop and fix Kurooto (being written for 0.5.8).
  • Use GBrain more: weekly checkup and contradiction reports in chat, entity and timeline pages for courses, queries during calls.
  • Make more add-ons public (the generic ones: backups, desktop helpers, habits, handwriting).
