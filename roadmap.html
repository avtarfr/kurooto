Roadmap

Ideas that aren't built yet. Nothing here is promised; each one ships as an opt-in feature through the normal signed updates when it's ready.

Next

  • Your notes, your layout. Choose where Kurooto writes: the daily note, subject folders, the inbox, your profile. It then fits an existing notes system instead of bringing its own.

From the skeptic review (2026-10-23)

Three skeptics checked the panel's ideas for a public release and looked for setup Kurooto could do itself.

  • Make the panel's ideas general: link recall cards and ~~the revision plan~~ (0.5.16) also draw on course notes and Classroom files, not only books; the meeting brief is for any recurring meeting ("With: X", their role in your own word: guide, manager, coach); "check my attempt" becomes "check my draft" (essays, accounts working, code, proofs); missed-class catch-up works without Classroom.
  • A Research add-on for the narrow ones: notation crosswalk, run compare, LaTeX tracker, proof checking. Bedside mode is an Android add-on.
  • ~~No college-term defaults: the check-in times and Start-by's day window are learned from when you actually reply (with a notice and Undo), not 09:00-22:00; a first-run question (student, working, research, other) picks the features; public holidays come from your country's calendar.~~ (0.5.9: learned check-in times and window, "What do you do?" in the wizard, holiday calendars)
  • ~~The phone app as a full chat channel beside Telegram, for places where Telegram is blocked or little used.~~ (0.5.16: Android's chat, handled like a Telegram message with working buttons; notify.app_chat sends Kurooto's own messages there too) (0.5.18: the iPhone app's chat; Telegram is optional: setup offers the phone app instead, and without Telegram everything comes to the chat, files as Drive links or a sealed copy on the call line)
  • Small gaps: ~~add_deadline takes the course, so a mail's "Lab report" lands on the right course page~~ (0.5.15: course by name or short name; an unknown one is said, never guessed); a recurring fact said in chat becomes a class, a recurring quiz or a routine instead of being dropped; the nightly look back can propose routines and acts on data changes with Undo instead of waiting for a tap; project notes keep "With:", "last met" and "## Next steps"; subject aliases are learned from how you name them.
  • ~~Routines for people who aren't students: gym, shifts, standups, tuition; they hold calls like a class does.~~ (0.5.9: Commitments, /busy, also set up from what you say)
  • Never without a tap: setting or moving an alarm, placing calls, reading new mail senders, sending anything out, attaching a code repository to a project. Repositories are never searched for on the disk.

Ideas

  • Dream RSI, part three. The nightly replay that tunes when messages come (part two) applied to Kurooto's other recorded decisions: which effort an AI request gets, the order of fallback AIs when one fails, and how Start-by sizes work. Each candidate is replayed against what actually happened, the current rules always among them, and a change is kept only if it's better, one at a time, with Undo. After part two has proven itself.
  • Offline fallback. When the internet drops, a small model on your computer answers quick things and queues the rest.
  • An email-in address. Forward a notice or a receipt to your own call line, and it's filed in your notes or becomes a deadline.
  • Zotero. A paper from the research digest goes into Zotero with one tap, and reading notes link to it.
  • Photo → dates. Share a photo of a notice or a timetable from your phone, and the events and deadlines are pulled out.
  • Shared class lists. Friends in the same course can opt in to share deadlines and exam dates.
  • Quick-ask hotkey. A small prompt window on the desktop, on every system.
  • Location reminders on Android. "When I reach the library", without draining the battery.
  • Course notes to PDF. A LaTeX template for course notes.
