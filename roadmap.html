Roadmap

Ideas that aren't built yet. Nothing here is promised; each one ships as an opt-in feature through the normal signed updates when it's ready.

Next

  • ~~Your notes, your layout. Choose where Kurooto writes: the daily note, subject folders, the inbox, your profile. It then fits an existing notes system instead of bringing its own.~~ (0.5.20: the setup page's Notes step, also Settings → notes and kurooto setup notes, reads the folder without AI (daily-note names and folder, link style, header keys, subject folders, inbox) and proposes notes.layout + notes.paths with a confidence per line and a preview; Kurooto's own notes move as one plan shown first, with Undo from a move log. ~~Not yet: writing markdown links instead of wikilinks for folders that use them.~~ (0.6.0: notes.links wiki or markdown, proposed by the Notes step; every reader takes both))
  • Brain (Kurooto's mind), planned for 0.8.0. One feature, off by default, with a switch for every part: a tamper-evident log of its own decisions and a separate scorekeeper; one gate every message passes through, which may only make a message quieter, later or gentler; a read of how your weeks go, scored before it's trusted; drives and named states that change what it does (it never claims to feel, and never rewards itself for your attention); autonomy earned per kind of action, with the always-ask list never unlocking; a journal checked line by line against the log; a hidden test set it can't see, so its improvement is measured; overnight experiments on your projects; skills from solved tasks; week plans; and forgetting that really forgets. Turned off, Kurooto behaves exactly as 0.7.

  • ~~Missed-class catch-up.   • ~~Add-ons by link. kurooto addons add <git url>: a manifest card first, consent for code, pinned to a commit.~~ (0.6.0: also on the dashboard; held to its card (features, commands, AI tools, jobs, and only the hosts and programs it names); update shows what changes and asks again; remove with Undo)

  • ~~No college-term defaults: the check-in times and Start-by's day window are learned from when you actually reply (with a notice and Undo), not 09:00-22:00; a first-run question (student, working, research, other) picks the features; public holidays come from your country's calendar.~~ (0.5.9: learned check-in times and window, "What do you do?" in the wizard, holiday calendars)
  • ~~The phone app as a full chat channel beside Telegram, for places where Telegram is blocked or little used.~~ (0.5.16: Android's chat, handled like a Telegram message with working buttons; notify.app_chat sends Kurooto's own messages there too) (0.5.18: the iPhone app's chat; Telegram is optional: setup offers the phone app instead, and without Telegram everything comes to the chat, files as Drive links or a sealed copy on the call line)
