Capture

About five minutes at the computer, through the microphone Kurooto listens with. Each sample is checked before it counts; the sound is dropped as soon as it's checked. Never by voice: start it here or by typing /voice enrol.

    Train

    Training here is a personal template and thresholds over a pretrained speaker model: the average of your samples, and thresholds calibrated on your own samples against about 1,750 strangers' voice prints that ship with Kurooto. No network is trained on this computer.

    Test

    One try at the computer: say anything (up to 15 seconds).

    Improve over time

    Your voice print

    Start over records a new voice print from the beginning; the one you have keeps working until the new one is built, and Undo (Train) puts it back.

    Model

    Changing the model means enrolling again: vectors from two models can't be compared, and your sound is never kept to compute them again.

    Install from a file

    For a computer without internet: the path of the model file (or a package's .whl) already on this computer. It's checked against its pinned checksum; nothing is uploaded.

    Forget

    Deletes your voice print and its key from this computer. Nothing of it was ever sent anywhere.

    Forget your voice? The voice print and its key are deleted; you'd enrol again to use Voice ID.
