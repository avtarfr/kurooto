      Use more than this computer suggests

      You know your computer better than the detection does. Pick another setup, or add a model of your own, and Kurooto installs it for you. It will tell you what you're going past (a model bigger than your graphics memory runs partly on the processor, slowly) but it won't refuse.

      Pick the AI that answers you

      The AI reads what you send and writes the answers. ★ is the easiest start. You can change it any time, and add a second one as a fallback for when the first hits its limit.

      Teach it about you

      ★ Recommended, and optional (Next skips it). Your other AI accounts already know a lot about you: your projects, how you work, what you keep asking for help with. Bring that here once, and Kurooto starts out knowing you instead of from nothing.

      1. Type which AI and account you're starting with (e.g. "ChatGPT personal"), then copy a prompt: Your main AI for the one you use most, Every other account for all the rest.
      2. Paste it into a new chat in that account. If it has memory or "search past chats" settings, turn them on first: it can only report what it can see.
      3. Do this in as many AI accounts as you can: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI, school and work accounts, old accounts too. Each one knows different things; the more reports, the better your profile.
      4. When it has answered (it ends with a few questions), you can answer them in that chat and ask it to update the report. Then copy its whole answer, or download it as a file, and add it below.
      5. When you've added them all, press Build my profile.
