What the board shows

SYSTEMS

The core in the middle is Kurooto itself. It beats faster when it's busier (each beat runs out through its rims), and the line beneath is its heartbeat.

Each cell around it is a feature that's on, in that feature's colour. It breathes faster and swells when the feature is busy, and flashes when something happens in it. The marks round it (short branches in the new design) show how busy it was over the last two minutes. A grey, dashed cell isn't running; "idle" under a name means its program runs now and then.

A dot along a nerve is something happening: travelling into the core, something arrived (a message, a sync); out to a cell, Kurooto is doing something with it (sending, ringing, writing). A ripple from the core is news that belongs to no one feature.

Point at a cell for its card; click it (or Tab to it and press Enter) for its menu: ping sends a pulse there and back, sync now and quiet (it keeps working but stops messaging you, with an undo) where the feature has them, its settings and its activity. Click the core to do something (Ctrl+K). Drag to turn the ring, scroll to zoom; both spring back.

The corners hold today, what's due, your AI and recent events, folded to one line; point at one to open it, or press L for all.

THE BEING

The board "being" (the panes menu, or Settings → look.board) draws Kurooto as one body, on what is really happening. The mind: sparks fire in the region that does each kind of work. The heart beats faster the busier Kurooto is, and blood carries work out to the organ doing it, and back when it reports. The senses: the eye is your screen and activity, the cochlea hearing (calls, voice), the cilia your phone in your hand, the canals its motion, the coil this computer. Memory is your notes, coloured by neighbourhood. Each point of light is a feature, on the organ that does its work.

Drag turns everything; with an organ open, drag on it turns that organ. Wheel zooms, right-drag pans. Click an organ to look into it, a part of it for its Brain parts, features and settings, a point of light for its feature, a note for its preview. Esc or empty space goes back. Mute stops Voice hearing and speaking.

The talk box (Alt+K) talks to Kurooto from any board, as on Telegram; Settings → look.talk_box turns it off.

NOTES

Each dot is a note, bigger the more links it has; lines are links. Colours are neighbourhoods: each course or subject folder is one, maps and projects are the hub, and any other note joins the one most of its links go to.

R lights up notes changed lately; / finds a note; double-click opens it; drag to move, scroll to zoom. Point at anything for its card.

STUDY MODE

Alt+M (or study in the corner) keeps only your notes and studying: the map large, a focus and break timer that holds calls and messages and logs the time to the subject, today's study, what's due and the subject's notes. Alt+M again puts the board back as it was.
