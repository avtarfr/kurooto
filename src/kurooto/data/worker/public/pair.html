Android: the Kurooto app (recommended)

It rings like a real phone call, even with the screen locked, and works on old phones too (Android 6 and newer).

  1. 1. Download the app
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. If your phone warns about unknown apps, allow it for your browser (Kurooto isn't on the Play Store).
  3. 2. Pair the app

No app? Use this page as an app instead (rings less reliably).

iPhone: add Kurooto to your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari (needs iOS 16.4 or newer).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Kurooto from your Home Screen (not from Safari); it finishes pairing there.

On iPhone, calls arrive as notifications you tap to answer: they can't ring like a phone call, ring on silent, or show on the lock screen as a call.
