# Landing page (avtarfr.github.io/kurooto)

Mode: Persuade. Audience chooses itself: research students first (largest option), people organising their lives,
tinkerers and developers. Job: understand how Kurooto fits a day, then install the beta willing to experiment, then
star the repository. Proof: the real screenshots and loop in installer/media, real feature behaviour, 1571 tests,
three OSes. Constraints: kuro palette fixed (dark and light themes as night and day), beta note "tried on only a few
computers", no invented users or numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one day, from before dawn to the next night, and the scroll is the clock: the circle from
Kurooto's own icon is the sun on each hour's horizon (one per section, since a single fixed sun would sit over the
words), rising in the hero and setting under the last field, while every feature appears at the hour it acts. Refuses the category
default of a dark hero with a laptop screenshot over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night is kuro (#0B0F0A, #10150E, #151B12, #1B2317) with off-white ink #F2EEE8; day is the light
theme (#F4F2EC ground and #EAE6DD, the brand's own alternate light ground, #172012 ink, #3F5626 accent); the moss accent (#6B8A42 / #A6C07A) is the sun and the one action. One shape,
the circle, at four scales: the giant sun, the image masks, the buttons (full round), the list bullets. Libre Franklin
700 display, Source Serif 4 text. Fields of flat colour change by hour; no lines, no cards in rows.

STORY: The visitor says who they are, watches a day re-time itself to their waking hour, sees a task carried from
07:00 to midnight, sees the real screens, learns it lives on their computer and forgets only when asked, reads the
honest beta answers, then copies the install line and stars the repo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pre-dawn kuro field. Nav row: wordmark left, four links and a live clock reading the page's hour
right. Across the top: h1 at 6-8vw on two lines, "An assistant that fits your day"; left below it one sentence,
then the chooser: three full-round options with Research student the largest and selected, and the action "Walk
through a day". Lower-right: the moss sun, 150vh across (120vh left a third of the first screen empty), rising from
the bottom edge and cropped, carrying a real morning message fragment (data-ui) at true size. The page ends at
night: the install field is kuro with the sun setting under it.

FORM: one-motif (taste draw seed 1168936815) carrying grounded candidate 1 of 7, "one day, wake to sleep", chosen by
the user as the pick; seed:0370979b9304ea42. Signature interaction: the wake-time slider re-times the day with
missed-class catch-ups appearing; signature motion: the sun's scroll-linked arc with the page's hour, plus the
shutter on the showcase screenshot.
