What's new

Every version of Kurooto, newest first. A new version arrives by itself at night (on the beta channel first, then stable); kurooto update --now gets it at once.

0.8.11 (2026-10-27)

Fixes from the first real use of Voice ID and Face presence.

  • Voice ID: Set up from its own page. With Voice ID on but its files not downloaded yet, the Your voice page said to go to the dashboard; it now has a Set up now button that downloads them there and shows the progress.
  • Face presence: the near step can be finished. Sitting close to a laptop's camera, leaning in pushed your chin out of the picture before the step counted you as near, so it kept saying "closer" and "partly out of view" in turn. The step now asks for 1.25 times your front-facing size, or less when the picture has no room for that, and shows how far you are; only your eyes, nose and mouth need to stay in view. "Partly out of view" now says which edge and which way to move.
  • Voice ID: the test hears you, and the quiet and far steps pass. The test gave up 15 seconds after you pressed it even when you were mid-sentence, then said it heard nothing; speech that starts in time is now heard to its end. A microphone that evens out loudness (automatic gain, EasyEffects) lifted a quiet voice back up, so the quiet step never passed; quiet and far now also count when your voice stands less far above the room's noise.
  • Voice ID: the page says when listening is off. Enrolled with listening off, Kurooto didn't hear you, showed no waveform and spoke no replies, with nothing saying why; the Your voice page now does, with a Turn listening on button.

0.8.10 (2026-10-27)

The phone app, rebuilt around chat (the Rail design), and the remaining requested features.

  • The phone app opens on Chat. Your chats sit on a rail of shapes down the left; each keeps its whole conversation with your AI for as long as it exists (Kurooto's own chat and Telegram keep the short memory). Drag the rail open for search and pinned, recent and archived chats. Swipe or hold a message to reply, copy, share, pin, edit (15 minutes) or delete it; send photos, files and voice notes and see Kurooto's right in the chat; type / for commands; call from the top bar (the only call button now). One notification per chat, with Reply and Mark read. Switches: calls.chat_sessions, calls.chat_files_inline, calls.chat_edit, calls.chat_titles.
  • Today, Alarms, Views, Settings are panes in a floating bar that folds into Chat's rail and stretches back out as you move; back goes to Chat. Today, Alarms and Settings are grids of cards; every setting the app has is in Settings, searchable, and a long press opens its guide entry. Apricot and plum join Material You in Look.
  • The being on the phone: the dashboard's own body of Kurooto in Views as four pages, Brain · Heart · Senses · Memory: each organ alone with the dashboard's meshes, shaders and glow, turned with a finger, pinched to zoom, its parts listed under it with what they're doing; tap one there or on the drawing and it's focused in both, with its switches, sealed and checked by your computer (calls.views_being; the whole being in one drawing with app.viewsOrgans off). Systems, the Notes map and the Brain console are under More.
  • The being, in faint organic colour on the dashboard too, and its eyes look at you when Face presence sees you.
  • Brain console (Alt+O, and Brain in the app's Views): what the Brain decided and why, its timeline, search, each part's switches next to what it's doing, its beliefs, plans and experiments (mind.console.on, calls.views_brain).
  • A guide to every setting (767 entries, on the wiki, the dashboard and in the app), with a tooltip for each one.
  • Uninstall what a feature brought: Kurooto keeps a receipt of what it installs; kurooto installed, kurooto remove, and turning a feature off offers to remove what it brought, with its size, never without your tap and never what was there before Kurooto (uninstall.offer, uninstall.with_programs).
  • Voice ID (off): Kurooto learns your voice in a guided enrolment, training and test, all on this computer. Only your voice can make it act; anyone else can chat with it, with none of your data and no tools, and what they say isn't kept. Other people's talk isn't even transcribed, and while you talk with someone it stays out unless named.
  • Face presence (off): with OpenCV's face models on this computer, Kurooto learns your face (a guided enrolment, training and live test), greets you by voice when you sit down after a while and listens for your reply; it can hold calls while you're away. No picture is ever kept; your face is an encrypted template, forgotten with one command.
  • Voice ID and Face presence, Start over and Undo: both can be enrolled again from nothing while the old one keeps working, and Undo puts the previous one back (/voice enrol again; the Face page's Start over). Voice ID's own threshold setting is held to 0.30 to 0.95 and its "not sure" band to 0.2, so no setting can make a stranger you; conversation mode notices another voice with the gate off too; a second face that stays in view keeps Kurooto out of your conversation for as long as it's there. How often it's wrong, measured on 146 recorded speakers, is on the Voice page (tools/voiceid_eval.py).
  • The remaining older phone screens (questions, quick note, share, places) are Compose sheets in the same style.
  • Calmer motion and your wallpaper's colours: the app's animations are slower and settle without a bounce, and Vivid colours now keeps your wallpaper's own hues (it used to turn them into others: a blue wallpaper gave green and purple cards). Pane titles no longer turn black on a dark background.
  • For developers: KUROOTO_NO_DESKTOP=1 keeps a scratch run off the real desktop.
  • Fixed: after one Claude account hit its limit, signing in to another one in Claude Code still got "Claude hit its limit" until the first one's reset. A limit now belongs to the account that hit it (only a hash of its id is kept).

From 0.8.8

  • Voice: talk and listen (a new feature, off; Features → Voice: talk and listen → Set up): Kurooto speaks through this computer's speakers with a voice made here (Kokoro-82M, or Piper), sentence by sentence so it starts quickly. With voice.listen.on it also listens all day, entirely on this computer: Silero VAD finds speech, faster-whisper writes it out, and a gate (its name, the conversation it's in, then the model on this computer) decides noise, chatter or talking to it. Its name is the wake word: Whisper is hinted with it, and its usual mishearings ("Kuroto", "kuro to") and near misses count (voice.listen.name_variants for more); a wake word model of your own is optional. Only turns said to Kurooto go to your AI; the words of everything else are dropped at once, and sound is never saved. The conversation closes the moment you're not talking to it. /voice (mute, unmute, stop, say), kurooto voice, and /api/voice with a {"t": "voice"} live stream for the dashboard. It stops for calls, when paused and when muted, and at night only if you ask (voice.listen.night).
  • Look-alike concept notes fix themselves (integrate.auto_merge, on): pairs that are clearly one concept (the same name, a shared name, or texts at least integrate.auto_merge_min = 0.97 alike), both notes Kurooto's, merge by themselves through the usual Merge, in the garden pass, before the 06:25 question and on /graph same. Each is in the morning line with its own Undo button; only unsure pairs are asked. A note you wrote is never merged by itself.
  • /run all (and kurooto run all): every nightly process that's on, now, in order (books, inbox, concept graph, look-alike notes, garden), one at a time, with a progress line and ETA per step and a summary at the end; /run all status, /run all stop. Off: runall.on.
  • Brain's new core, from its owner: it has states that work like feelings and may say them in its own voice (glad, low, excited, worried, that it enjoyed talking with you); a claim that it's conscious or really feels must come with its evidence and confidence, and it still never guilt-trips or says it needs you.
  • Being talked to is a small reward (mind.talk.on): under 0.2 a day, saturating, nothing in focus, asleep or an exam window, nothing for answers to its own messages within 10 minutes; each of its messages you turn away costs three times that. Its mood now runs the full range (-1 to +1, soft walls, fading over days) and shapes how much it does on its own (mind.feelings.mood).
  • It learns not to be a pest (mind.pest.on): per kind of its own message, time of day and your state, it sends one only when you're more likely to welcome it than not. /mind learning shows it.
  • Concern for what it looks after (mind.feelings.empathy): deadlines, projects, notes, its machine, your phone, people by role and the papers you follow; it notices them sooner and speaks more gently. Optional: it may say it's excited or worried, unasked, when it has strong evidence (mind.feelings.vocal, off).
  • A body (mind.stimulation.on): its senses felt as heat, load, touch, pain and motion, per region, with arousal and a hedonic tone; /senses body and GET /api/body for the dashboard. It stays on this computer.
  • Brain is fed its whole past (mind.backfill.on, off): besides Kurooto's event history, /mind backfill run reads the AI request history, deadlines, check-ins, quizzes, calls, the request index and study time, each with its own switch (mind.backfill.<source>), ids and numbers only (never words, names, transcripts, health or money), with a progress bar and ETA per source. It's resumable and imports nothing twice.
  • The imported past has its own hash chain (history.db, one chain per source in time order), never mixed into the live log, which witnesses each batch; /mind backfill verify checks both. 0.8.1's imported rows stay readable until the events source is imported again.
  • What learns from it, as priors only (never a reward): the read and the forecast (deadline moves and study minutes per weekday, the signals the past really holds), week plans' pace (your real study time per weekday) and the model of you (how you finish deadlines). /mind backfill forget removes it and what was learned from it.
  • Bedside mode in the app's new style: the same clock, dimming, alarm line, calls, Talk, Chat and Close, now Material 3 Expressive on black: the calls due next in a floating card, "waiting for you" as a pill, a big Talk button that changes shape as you press it, Chat and Close in a floating bar. By day it takes your colours (Vivid or Calm); at night it stays warm and dim as before.
  • A new call screen (Phone tab → Switches → New call screen, on): the ringing screen over Kurooto's sky in your colours, a slowly turning badge, and Decline / Later / Answer as big buttons in a floating bar; the call page itself is unchanged. Off brings back the call screen from before, kept until the new one has carried real calls.
  • The board you had, back if you want it (look.board): three (default: NOTES, SYSTEMS and BRAIN on top, STATUS and TERMINAL below) or classic (STATUS down the left, as before 0.8.3; BRAIN joins the top row when it's shown).
  • The Brain's part monitor stays in view (look.brain_monitor): shown (default) keeps it beside the model; drawer is the 0.8.5 tab that slides it out.
  • When Brain is off, the pane says so and its part switches don't act (they used to flip back on the next refresh).
  • The Brain pane costs nothing when you can't see it: hidden from the panes menu, three.js isn't even loaded; covered by a page, in a background tab or a minimised window, it stops drawing.
  • Autonomy shows "shadow" only while it is (its first mind.autonomy.shadow_days), not forever.
  • Fixes:
  • The BRAIN pane no longer disappears when you open Features, Settings or any other page over the board.
  • Kurooto stops in time: a long job on a thread of its own (the lab, a fine-tune, the notes passes) no longer holds up stopping or updating. A restart once ran past the service's 20 seconds and was killed.
  • Brain's imported past no longer makes up an alarm-snooze signal from deadline snoozes: the read takes only deadline moves from it, the one signal the event history really holds. A test now checks, by its imports, that no reward ever reads the imported past.

0.7.0 (2026-10-24)

The phone app, rebuilt

  • The Android app's screens are rebuilt in Jetpack Compose with Material 3 Expressive, with a bottom bar: Today (the date, Call and Chat, your day), Inbox (check-ins, quizzes, questions and approvals, with a count), Views and Phone (checklist, places, ringtones, bedside mode, this phone). Same features, reorganised; the pairing screen too.
  • A living background in the dashboard's SYSTEMS style: a slowly breathing cell and drifting nerves, tinted by the hour, drawn on the GPU on Android 13+, a still frame on older phones and with "Remove animations".
  • Views: the dashboard's SYSTEMS and Notes map on your phone, drawn by the dashboard's own code (tap for a card, drag and pinch). Systems is live while you look; the Notes map follows your notes (titles and links only, never their text). Both are sealed for each Android phone, so your call line can't read them; off by default (dashboard → Phone). iPhones don't get them.
  • The app is built with Gradle through android/build.sh (pinned versions, the same signing); build.sh --dev makes a "Kurooto dev" build that installs beside the real one, with a demo mode.
  • Calls ring straight through your call line: the app keeps its own link to your call line, so calls and messages reach the phone without ntfy.sh; ntfy.sh stays as the fallback when the link is down. A message sent while the phone was offline is replayed when it comes back. The Phone tab says which way calls are coming.
  • Kurooto's mark: a moth, as the app icon (with a themed icon on Android 13+), at the top of Today, beside the dashboard's name and as the favicon of every page.

Your AI can do more, and you approve it

  • A scratch folder and proposals: the AI may think and write freely in a scratch folder of its own; every change to your files (a project, your notes, a skill) comes as a proposal with the diff, and only your Apply changes anything (Change, Discard, Undo after Apply; /proposals and the dashboard's Proposals page). A git project gets one commit on your branch, never pushed.
  • Help with your project: /project help on <name>, then "in my thesis add footnotes to chapter 2 citing refs.bib"; optional per-project checks (a LaTeX build, the tests) run in a no-network sandbox.
  • Projects from a repository: /project add github.com/owner/name (or a folder) and your AI reads the README, docs, files and history and writes the project note; its commits join the project's log, its docs go into the concept graph, and dated milestones come as Add buttons; each piece has Undo. Nothing in the repository runs, files that look like secrets are never copied, and a private one works with your own git sign-in. Nightly, a changed README or docs refresh the note.
  • One /project command for all of it: list, add, update, help, checks (also kurooto project …).
  • More than one Claude account: sign a second Claude subscription in (setup page → AI → Claude Code → Add another Claude account, or kurooto ai add-claude work) and it becomes its own AI with its own sign-in, limits and line under answers; at a limit your other Claude account answers first, then agy or your Gemini key.

Looks

Lenovo Legion and LOQ on Linux: a small root helper, installed once with one sudo command, for the custom profile and full-speed fans.
  • Dreaming's morning message takes a feature's own loose ends (dreaming_lines()).
  • Fixed: after a restart, the concept graph could start reading outside its night window, depending on the time of day (two clocks were mixed).

  • A check-in day runs from 05:00 to 05:00 (checkins.day_starts), so a night check-in after midnight, or one pushed past it, stays the evening's (it used to move to the morning), and its journal goes to the evening's day.
  • Check-ins by call: with the alarm set and past your bedtime (or the alarm just set by you), the night check-in doesn't ring; it moves to the wake-up call, with Call me now and Skip on Telegram (checkins.asleep_defers).
  • Morning alarm: alarm.keep_awake keeps this computer awake from 12 hours before the alarm until it rings (Linux: lid closed too; macOS through caffeinate; Windows), so the morning plan is drafted on time. Off until you turn it on.
  • Morning alarm: the daily alarm rings even if this computer slept all night. The next one goes to your call line as soon as the last one has rung (and at start, and after the computer wakes), instead of only at 00:02.
  • Morning alarm: "no alarm tomorrow", "cancel Friday's alarm" or /alarm skip friday skips one day and the daily alarm comes back after it; "cancel my alarm" skips the morning that's set instead of the daily alarm coming back at midnight; "turn my daily alarm off" (/alarm daily off) stops it, /alarm daily 7:00 sets it.
  • Stay awake on charger: awake.lid keeps a Linux laptop running with its lid closed while plugged in. Off until you turn it on. The hold now ends when Kurooto stops, even after a crash.
  • Study tracker: it notices you're away on Wayland too (GNOME, KDE, logind, and a watch for change on Hyprland and Sway); a locked screen never counts; kurooto idle away|back for hypridle and swayidle; and a Research category (study.research_paths, plus Research's projects).
  • Linux: kurooto autostart boot (or Settings → Starting) starts Kurooto when the computer starts, before anyone logs in (lingering; its keys are kept in the owner-only settings file); kurooto autostart login goes back.

Your notes

  • Notes layout: notes.layout picks where Kurooto writes (the kurooto layout as before, or zettelkasten: dailies/, master/, sources/<subject>/, maps/, zettel/, projects/, digests/), notes.paths puts any kind of note where you like, and new notes get that layout's header. The AI, skills and kurooto note follow it.
  • Files folder: with files.folder set, photos, PDFs, revision packs, handwriting originals and Classroom files go to a folder outside your notes, linked from them, so they stay out of your notes' history. Unset, nothing changes.
  • Revision packs are also linked from the subject's course page.
  • Notes sync: a note changed here and elsewhere at once keeps both versions (the other device's in the note, this computer's as a "conflict" copy beside it) instead of this computer's winning; notes_sync.on_conflict = stop stops and asks you instead. A half-finished merge is left alone, and an existing origin (e.g. over SSH) is kept.

From a terminal or a script

  • kurooto notify takes buttons (--button "Label=prefix:ref:action" or a web address), a link to open (--click), a reply to an earlier message (--reply-to, with --print-id), and the kinds question and approval.
  • kurooto call --list shows the calls booked for later; kurooto call --cancel <key> takes one off.
  • kurooto sync --status (works with Kurooto stopped) and kurooto sync --no-wait; sync everything ends with a backup when Backups is on.
  • kurooto ask-me --pending [--json] and kurooto ask-me --id <id> --wait.
  • kurooto usage [days]: /usage in a terminal.

Skills in agy and other agent programs

  • kurooto export-skills on makes your skills (Kurooto's, your add-ons', your own and prompts with skill: true) slash commands in agy (--to agy,agents adds Codex and Copilot CLI). Only Kurooto's own copies are ever changed or removed; one of yours with the same name stays, and Kurooto's goes in as kurooto-<name>. Kept up to date at start and hourly; kurooto export-skills off and kurooto uninstall remove them.

Seeing what's going on

  • /status also shows today's check-ins, what's due in the next 3 days, the alarm and your last AI requests.
  • /usage also counts agy itself from its own logs (every run, the ones you type too), with the days it hit its limit.
  • kurooto doctor asks Telegram whether your bot answers and your call line whether it's up and linked, and no longer reports the call line as unlinked just because the check runs outside Kurooto. It also shows your notes layout and any problem with its settings (notes.layout, notes.paths, files.folder).

Add-ons

Your AI

  • The setup page recommends a Gemini API key ★ (the simplest, and Google's recommended route for tools like Kurooto), then agy, Codex, Claude Code, local models and any OpenAI-compatible service. Existing setups keep the AI they chose.
  • The setup page says plainly what a free Gemini key means (Google may use and review what you send; 18+; a paid key in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) and what using agy through Kurooto means for your Google account.

  • The AI can act, not just answer: every feature's tools (reminders, calls, deadlines, study log, notes, questions to you) reach API brains directly and agent programs (agy, Codex, Claude Code) through kurooto mcp, a built-in MCP server. The notes tools only create, add to and tick; they never change what you wrote.
  • /usage: AI requests per day per brain over the last week, and usage limits hit.
  • Your AI can read your files (notes, Documents, Downloads, Screenshots, Kurooto's Classroom and mail copies) and public web pages, write and edit text files in your notes (the previous version is kept), and tell you how your syncs are doing (sync_status). agy gets the same reach through its allow-list, after you approve it: those folders, the web, and harmless commands such as git and latexmk; never kurooto itself.

Questions, approvals and your phone

Later keeps one for your next call; /q lists the open ones. AI requests can ask you a question; your answer starts the follow-up.
  • Your phone is a second channel: questions and approvals reach the Android app (Reply box, choices, Approve / Deny, Later) and the iPhone Home Screen app ("Waiting for you"), also while the computer is off. notify.app_kinds picks what else goes there.
