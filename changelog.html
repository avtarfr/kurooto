What's new

Every version of Kurooto, newest first. A new version arrives by itself at night (on the beta channel first, then stable); kurooto update --now gets it at once.

0.9.3 (2026-10-29)

  • Echo cancelling (voice.listen.aec, auto: on while Kurooto plays on an add-on's output that also hears; "on" for this computer's speakers too; "off"). What it plays (its voice, the heard-you sound, Gemini Live's answers) is taken out of the microphone's sound before barge-in, the VAD and Voice ID hear it, with the system's speexdsp library (echo canceller and residual echo suppressor, through ctypes; nothing installed). The delay is measured while it plays and followed sentence by sentence (an add-on's measured delay seeds it); barge-in then expects only what's left of the echo, so you can cut in over a phone whose speaker is louder in its own microphone than you are. Without the library: "unavailable" in /voice and kurooto doctor, listening as before. Numbers only (voice_aec: delay, ERLE).
  • A tool call on a Live turn no longer falls back to the voice made here (a fix): the wait for Live's first sound stops while the tool runs and starts again in full once its result is sent; the turnComplete Live sends with the call isn't taken as the end, and the heard-you sound plays again meanwhile.
  • "Others nearby" doesn't flicker on a faint detection (a fix): only a face Face presence would judge (its score and minimum size) counts toward others, being alone and the face sizes, and "others nearby" needs a second face in 2 of the last 3 frames (off after 2 frames without one).
  • The local judge says when it can't run (a fix): the offline helper times a changed model by itself (its speed test was for an old one, so unclear statements after a look were dropped quietly for days), and /voice and doctor say when the judge is set up but unusable.
  • Your own "not sure" voice isn't a second voice (a fix): through a microphone Voice ID knows less well, a turn scored above the guest line with only your face (or none) in view doesn't make the careful calls or conversation mode treat it as someone else.
  • "How are you? Why do you feel low?" gets a real answer (mind.tools.feelings, on with Brain's feelings). A read-only tool, brain_feelings, for your AI, calls and desk Live: Kurooto's own feeling and what it's about ("uneasy about something I said I'd do: remind you to book the lab slot"), why (the promise, where and when it said it, and whether it's since been kept), what it's doing about it (when it rechecks) and its top concerns ("Organic Chemistry: Quiz (due in 22 hours)"). Calls and Live are told to use it for these questions. Only its own state: never its read of you, your mood or tone, its forecast of you, health or money, and no scores.
  • A promise to remind is kept once the reminder exists (a fix). When Kurooto said it would remind you of something and that reminder (or a deadline for it) is then set, the promise counts as kept, and its feeling is worked out again within a minute of the reminder or deadline being added or changed, not only on its next timer.

0.9.2 (2026-10-29)

  • Gemini Live for every turn at the desk (voice.listen.live_everyone, off; with voice.listen.live): every turn said to Kurooto goes to Live, face seen or not. Yours (Voice ID) get your session with its tools; anyone else's, and yours Voice ID is unsure of, get a separate guest session with no tools and none of your data, counted against the guest hourly cap. Other people's voices go to Google with it on.
  • A new microphone or camera is noticed (a fix): Voice ID and Face presence remember which microphone and camera they were enrolled with, and say once when most of your turns or frames aren't recognised through another one (/voice enrol, Add this look).
  • Desk Live fixes: a tool's result is never said or shown as the answer (a read it did before failing goes the usual way); talking over an answer stops it at once (it waited out a 12-second hold) and stops Live's answer at Google's end; cutting in works when a phone both plays and hears; Live's sound reaches a phone's speaker in pieces of about a second (no cut-outs), with an optional gapless hook for outputs (ahead_s); enrolment never keeps its own spoken steps as your samples.
  • Desk chat turns no longer notify on the phone (a fix): quiet messages and the Desk chat are added silently.
  • Voice ID learns each microphone you use (voice.speaker_id.per_device, on). Through a microphone you didn't enrol on (the Body phone's, a headset), turns proven yours (only your face in view, one voice, 2 s) build that microphone's own print, calibrated against strangers heard through a simulated phone channel, and it asks once before using it. Vectors only, sealed with your voice print; /voice id mics. A fix with it: "improve over time" no longer learns through a microphone without its own print (it drifted your print until it stopped for good).
  • "That was me" (voice.speaker_id.claim, on). Tap the Body face within 10 s of a "not sure" turn, with only your face in view: it's heard again as yours. At most 5 a day; the turn's sound waits in memory for those 10 s only.
  • Face presence doesn't flicker (a fix). A face far smaller than the biggest in view no longer counts as someone here, someone else needs two of their last three frames, and your face stays yours for about two seconds through a blurred, unsure or misjudged frame, so "only you in view" (Voice ID's face assist, look to talk) holds steady.
  • Room modes: alone, company, night, silent (voice.room_modes.on). /room night (Telegram, the app's chat, the dashboard), the room button and Hearing panel on the dashboard, Today's Room card in the phone app, "night mode" in your own voice (Voice ID; voice.room_modes.spoken), or the Body face. Company keeps your things quiet, night is also quieter (voice.room_modes.night_db), silent is on screen only. A mode only tightens, never turns a feature on, and ends by itself.
  • One output stage for every sound (a fix): Kokoro, Piper, the "heard you" sound and Gemini Live are levelled to about the same loudness and held under -3 dBFS.
  • Ears source lost (voice.degrade.ears, off): when the microphone you named goes away, it listens through this computer's own within about two seconds, with that microphone's voice print, and goes back. Kokoro fails (voice.degrade.piper, on): Piper says the rest, only if it's already set up.
  • Repeating reminders (reminders.repeat, on): "remind me every day at 22:15 to call home", "on weekdays at 7", "every Monday and Thursday at 9 until 4 dec", also on calls and through /api/act (repeat, until). /reminders lists each once with Cancel and Skip next. A call is told never to say a reminder repeats unless the tool said so (one promised "every day" when only tonight's was set).
  • "Make it 10:15" changes the reminder just set (change_reminder, calls and AI requests) instead of adding a second; a bare time keeps its day and takes the reading nearest the old time. A second add_reminder with the same words within 15 minutes moves the first.
  • Calls ask one thing at a time. A tool's result that arrives while a question waits is added quietly instead of starting a second question over it, and two things the call says in a row are two transcript lines, not one run together.
  • An after-class question already answered on the call isn't asked again: a class whose quiz was talked about is one question, and after the call one still open for a class the call went over is answered with your words (or closed after "I already answered that").
  • One greeting: a call Kurooto places fetches what it starts from while it connects, so it no longer says "Hi, " and then greets again.
  • Spoken turns stall less (fixes): a voice Voice ID is unsure of is written out once, not twice; the transcriber and Kokoro use a fixed number of threads; an AI program doing automatic work runs at a lower priority; a long first sentence is said from its first clause. A turn whose words took too long can say "Sorry, I lost you there. Say that again?" instead of answering late (voice.listen.stage_deadline_s, off), and it can wait for the rest when your words stop on "and" or a comma (voice.listen.wait_unfinished, off).
  • Every turn's numbers (voice.listen.trace, on): /voice turns shows the last 20 (the gate, the face, Voice ID, the way it went, each stage's time); the dashboard's hearing panel shows p50 / p90 to the first sound.
  • Faster Gemini Live at the desk: the fast way (voice.listen.fast_live, off) opens Live while your face looks at the camera and talks and judges your words at your first pause (about 0.8 s sooner with stand-ins); its connection opened ahead on your face or wake word (voice.listen.live_prewarm, off); "Live resting" after failed connections (voice.live.breaker.on), a heard-you sound (voice.live.ack), context kept for 10 minutes (voice.live.resume) and a cap on how long it talks (voice.live.spoken_max_s), all off.

The call skips only a class you name or clearly mean ("today's class", said about a class it just asked about, is that class) and says back which ones it skipped.
  • The wake-up call's questions about yesterday say "yesterday" (a fix). A check-in moved to the wake-up call asks "anything for Wednesday's journal? Only your answer to the journal question and what you said about that day's classes go into that day's journal; the new day's planning stays out of it.

  • Done deadlines stay off the morning plan (a fix): one ticked off in the last day and a half is never brought up as a to-do on the wake-up call, even when the plan drafted before it or a note still lists it.
  • One question, once a call (a fix): an answered question, even an unclear answer, isn't asked again in the same call.
  • The Desk chat (calls.chat_desk, on): what you say at your desk (aloud, a Live talk, the board's talk box) and the answers, as one chat named Desk on the dashboard's Chat page and the phone app, without a notification. Your turns only. calls.chat_desk_history (off) lets the talk box follow on from it.
  • Talk-box answers aren't lost when the page closes (a fix): an AI answer to the talk box arrives in the Desk chat and on the "while you were away" card, never on Telegram.
  • The Waiting page (look.being_held_link, on; Alt+W): click the being's held count to read every held message in full, with Send now.
  • Calls at the desk first (calls.desk_first, off): a call Kurooto places by itself is offered aloud at your desk when you're there alone; yes takes it there, no or silence rings the phone as before.   • Reminders and questions said aloud at the desk (voice.speak.messages, off): while you're alone at the desk, as well as the usual copies; your own spoken answer closes a question everywhere, and "done" takes a reminder down. Never approvals. Features and add-ons share one spoken-answer helper (Voice.spoken_answer, spoken_yes).
  • While you were away (face.away.summary, off): back at the desk, one pop-up and a card listing what came meanwhile; face.away.summary_only (off) shows only that pop-up until you press Send now.

0.9.0 (2026-10-29)

  • Handwritten notes say what's already in your notes (handwritten.overlap, on; handwritten.overlap_courses limits it to the courses you list). After a written-out note is linked, one AI request compares its sections with the course's folder, its earlier handwritten notes and the concept notes: repeated sections get "(already in [[note]])" on their heading, and the note gets an "Already in your notes" and a "New here" list. Only Kurooto's parts of the new note change; nothing is deleted. /handwritten undo takes it back with the linking.
  • "Only from <topic>" (handwritten.only_from, on): a text file next to the scan, "from <topic>" in its name, or /handwritten only <file or course> from <topic> adds the part of the note from that heading on (a loose match), with the marks. The full transcription stays.
  • Study notes from an upload, as one request (study.notes_workflow, on). "Make notes from my AN2 notes from functions of several variables, PDF in Downloads/Study" now runs start to end: your AI finds the scan, reads it now with the handwritten reader (its own settings: the model here, or Gemini only when allowed), cuts it from that topic on, leaves out what your notes already have, builds a study pack for the rest, writes deep notes as LaTeX by the pack's prompt, compiles them, files the PDF and the .tex in the course's files folder with a source note linking them (never the notes' top folder), and sends you the PDF, telling you how it's going. Also /study-notes-from-upload.
  • Kurooto fixes its own LaTeX instead of saying it can't compile. Every compile checks the document first (unbalanced braces or environments, a package not loaded such as xcolor for a colour like blue!50!black in hyperref's options, [...] inside an optional argument, missing \input files), then answers with the errors and their lines; the AI fixes the source and tries again, up to three times, then tells you the error that's left.
  • Study packs take the right chapters of a book (a fix). A book's pages on a topic come from the chapters whose title or section headings name it, or that the class cites, not from the one that only says its words more often (a several-variables pack took the chapter on series of functions); neighbouring chapters stay together, and INDEX.md says which chapters were taken and why.
  • One person is one face (a fix). A smaller face box inside a bigger one (half a face, a reflection in glasses) is no longer counted as a second person, which kept "others nearby" flickering and made Kurooto careful with its name when you were alone.
  • "Chem hw is done" marks it done (a fix). Saying a deadline is done in your own words, even with a slip of the keyboard ("doje", "dun", "finsihed", "submited"), marks the one open deadline the words name, with Undo: homework words plus a course's name or short name find that course's nearest open homework, a number picks that one. When two fit as well, it asks which with a button each; nothing is marked without one clear match.
  • Your AI can find a deadline to tick off (a fix). AI requests (Claude Code, agy, an API key) get list_deadlines, each open deadline with its id, and mark_deadline_done and move_deadline also take words of the title and course instead of the id; when two fit, nothing changes and the AI asks you which. Ids are in the reserve's deadlines.md too.
  • Study mode keeps the camera on (face.watch.in_focus, on). Focus and a study timer no longer pause Face presence, so look to talk, the desk keeping Kurooto awake and the desk phone's eye go on while you study. Off: paused in focus, as before. (Listening never paused for focus.)
  • Add this look (face.looks.on, on). Face presence calls you "unsure" with your hair up or a hat, and learning never learns from unsure frames. Now the Face page's Add this look, /face add-look [label] or kurooto face add-look [label] takes about 10 seconds of you at the camera, alone, through enrolment's checks, and adds up to 10 samples as a labelled look of their own, listed with Remove (at most 6). A look too far from your enrolment to be sure it's you is refused with the reason: the threshold is never loosened for it. Numbers only, sealed in your template; typed or tapped only.
  • Calls know you as your AI does (calls.context_brief, on). A phone call and a Live talk at the desk now hear what every AI request is told: your profile's brief, your how-to-help-me note, your not-interested topics and the Brain's own state. Never your read, mood, tone or senses.
  • Your AI knows your not-interested list (notinterested.tell_ai, on). Every request, call and desk talk is told the topics (not your reasons), so it doesn't bring them up on its own.
  • More features can use the not-interested list (notinterested.filter.research, integrate, quiz_links, retro, frontier, each off). Turned on, a matching paper, class reading, link card, nightly idea or frontier card comes last, tagged "you said not interested in X". Nothing is hidden.
  • Call lessons reach every request (calls.lessons_everywhere, on). Each lesson from the look back after a call is also a dated line under "Learned on calls" in your how-to-help-me note; a replaced or forgotten lesson takes its line out too.
  • Calls know which senses are on (calls.tell_senses, on): Face presence and Voice ID, and when you were last at the desk. Words and a time only.
  • Calls can look more up (calls.read_agent_tools, on). Calls and desk Live talks also get your AI's read-only tools: the concept graph's search and cards, Books' pages, chat history search, a Classroom file's text.
  • A call says when the day changed under it. After a deadline, class, commitment or plan change, the tool's answer says the brief and today's plan are out of date there, and the phone's Today marks the plan.
  • What you say on a call counts (calls.after_infer, on): it's read like a typed message ("I got a project under Prof X", "remember that ..."). With tone on, your call lines count toward the day's tone too (mind.tone.calls).
  • "I sent it" closes a thread during the call (calls.threads_live, on).
  • Calls on the move (calls.mode_when_moving, off). A call Kurooto places by itself while your phone says you're walking or in a vehicle uses the phone's own speech. It never holds the call.
  • More on the phone's Today: the plan's first lines, what to start and hours studied (calls.today_plan); held messages with Send now, waiting proposals and the Brain's state (calls.today_waiting); whether the laptop's camera is on and why (face.phone_badge).
  • The read counts alarm snoozes and skipped class hours (mind.read.snoozes, mind.read.skipped_classes, on). A "Later" on the wake-up call now names the call it was on (a fix).
  • Add-ons and the desk greeting (each behind its switch). An add-on may add one short line to the greeting (face.greet.addons, off; said last, alone in view, never pushing a part out, and it tells the add-on whether it was said), and may say everyone else in view is someone it knows (face.addons.known_guests), so a remembered friend gets the short greeting instead of none; Face presence still never works out who anyone is.
  • The room's light from the camera (face.light_sense, off). While the camera looks anyway, the frames' average brightness becomes one smoothed number for Brain's body and add-ons; never a frame.
  • Your AI knows how a message came (chat.context_line, on). Typed on Telegram, in the phone app or on this computer, on a call, or that you stepped away from the desk and when, in plain words. Your phone's saved place and Wi-Fi name go along only with chat.context_places (off).
  • "Yes" to the desk greeting continues it (face.greet.tell_ai, on). Answering the greeting, your AI is told what Kurooto had just said.
  • "Why did you...?" gets the real reason (mind.why.to_ai, on; with Brain). A message that asks why carries the plain reasons for Kurooto's own last few messages, never a sense, a mood or a tone.
  • Greetings learn from how they went (face.greet.records, on; with Brain). Each desk greeting is a decision record (the parts it said, never the words) closed by whether you answered it, and the nightly look back may suggest a different gap between greetings from them, with your tap.

  • Your phone leaving home tells the desk (each off). With Brain's phone places sensor, your phone leaving a place you saved marks you away at once (face.away.use_places), so held calls and pop-ups wait sooner; the camera can stay closed while you're out (face.away.places_close_camera) and Voice can stop listening (voice.listen.phone_away). Only the fact and its time are used, never the place.
  • Who was at your desk while you were out (face.away.report_others, off). With Face presence noticing when you leave, it notes the minutes someone else's face was in view and tells you once you're back ("at 14:10 and 14:25 (2 faces)"). Times and counts only, in memory, then forgotten; never a picture, never who, never to your AI.
  • Words made by rules use your persona card (persona.templates, on). The desk greeting says the name you gave ("Welcome back, Sam.") while nobody else is in view, "I'm awake" answers "Good morning, Sam.", and the avoid line is honoured as punctuation (no exclamation marks, no emojis) in those and in check-in messages. No part of a greeting is dropped for it.
  • Look to talk can centre beside the lens (face.camera.yaw_offset_deg, 0). When the spot you're meant to look at sits beside the camera (a drawn eye next to a phone's camera), a look there counts, by the head's turn and by the eyes; an add-on with such a camera can set it while its camera is in use. At 0 nothing changes.
  • Send pictures and files from the dashboard's chat (dashboard.chat_attach, on). On the Chat page, paste a picture (Ctrl+V), drop files anywhere on the page, or pick them with the new paperclip; they wait above the box with a small preview and × to take one off, then go with your message (up to 10, 20 MB each). Your AI gets them as it gets a photo or a file on Telegram, a picture as a picture; they're kept in Kurooto's state folder, never your notes.
  • Photos sent to your AI reach it as pictures. A photo from Telegram, the phone app or the dashboard now goes with the request itself to an AI service that reads pictures (a Gemini key, an Ollama model with vision), instead of only its path, which such a service couldn't open.
  • What you're learning now, in quizzes and /revise (quiz.learning_now, exam_season.learning_now, on). When another feature marks concepts you keep coming back to, a quiz session ends with one extra card on one of them, and /revise <course> names them in a line above its list; the usual cards and the ranking are unchanged.

When an add-on says you're deep in one thing, what Kurooto starts by itself waits even at a break, never past max_hold_min, and goes when you come up; alarms, reminders, calls, approvals and close deadlines are never held.

  • Your voice and the dashboard say you're here (presence.owner_here.face, .voice, .dashboard, .phone, on). One words-free sign from whichever sense saw you: Brain's day counts an hour of talking at the desk or ticking deadlines on the board, the presence forecast sees you at the desk, using the dashboard or the phone app wakes a sleeping Kurooto as a tap does, and your voice ends Face presence's "stepped away" without a greeting.
